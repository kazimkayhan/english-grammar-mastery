# English grammar

## Words
### 1 Word classes & formation
- noun · verb · adjective · adverb
- pronoun · preposition · conjunction · determiner
- prefixes: un- re- dis-
- suffixes: -tion -ness -ly
- conversion: an email → to email
### 2 Nouns
- plural: books · children
- countable / uncountable
- compound: bus stop · a ten-minute walk
- possession: Sara's · the end of the film
### 3 Articles & determiners
- a / an · the · no article
- this · that · these · those
- some · any · much · many · few
- numbers & dates
### 4 Pronouns
- I · me · my · mine · myself
- something · anyone · nobody
- you / one / they (people in general)
### 5 Adjectives, adverbs & comparison
- a red car (adjective + noun)
- quickly · well · always
- bigger · the biggest · as … as
- very good · absolutely amazing
### 6 Prepositions
- place: in · on · at
- time: at 5 · on Monday · in May
- movement: into · across · through
- depend on · interested in

## Verbs
### 7 Tenses & aspect
- 3 times: past · present · future
- 4 aspects: simple · continuous · perfect · perfect continuous
- futures: will · going to · present continuous
- habits: used to · would
### 8 Modals
- ability: can · could · be able to
- permission: can · may
- obligation: must · have to · should
- deduction: must be · can't be · might have
### 9 Voice
- passive: It was built in 1990.
- causative: I had my car fixed.
- impersonal: He is said to be rich.
### 10 Verb patterns
- -ing or to: enjoy doing · want to do
- want sb to · make sb do
- phrasal verbs: give up · look after
- make / do / take + noun

## Sentences
### 11 Sentence structure
- word order: S + V + O
- questions & negatives (do-support)
- question tags: …, isn't it?
- there is / it is
- agreement: She works · They work
### 12 Clauses
- relative: who · which · that · whose
- noun: what you said · whether
- adverbial: when · because · although
- participle: Having finished, …
### 13 Conditionals
- 0 · 1 · 2 · 3 · mixed
- wish · if only · it's time
- inverted: Had I known…
### 14 Reported speech
- statements: She said (that)…
- questions: He asked if…
- commands: She told me to…
- reporting verbs: suggest · deny · admit

## Text
### 15 Linking, emphasis & information
- connectors: however · therefore · although
- ellipsis & substitution: so do I · I think so
- inversion: Never have I…
- clefts: What I need is…
- hedging: It seems that…
### 16 Style & mechanics
- formal vs informal
- subjunctive: I insist that he be…
- spoken & academic grammar
- US vs UK
- punctuation · spelling rules
