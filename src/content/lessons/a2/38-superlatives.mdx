## Comparing one with all the others

A **comparative** compares two things (<LessonRef>lesson 37 (Comparatives)</LessonRef>). A **superlative** compares one thing with **all** the others in a group: *Kabul is **the biggest** city in Afghanistan.*

<FormTable caption="How to make a superlative">

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| Short (one syllable) | cheap**er** | **the** cheap**est** |
| Two syllables, ending in **-y** | happ**ier** | **the** happ**iest** |
| Most other two-syllable and all long adjectives | **more** expensive | **the most** expensive |
| Irregular | better · worse · farther / further | **the best** · **the worst** · **the farthest / furthest** |

</FormTable>

## Short adjectives: the -est

The spelling rules are the same as for **-er** in lesson 37.

<FormTable caption="Spelling: the -est">

| Adjective ends in… | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| most letters | add **-est** | cheap → the cheapest · tall → the tallest · old → the oldest |
| **-e** | add **-st** | nice → the nicest · large → the largest · safe → the safest |
| one vowel + one consonant | **double** the consonant | big → the biggest · hot → the hottest · thin → the thinnest |
| consonant + **-y** | y → **-iest** | happy → the happiest · easy → the easiest · busy → the busiest |

</FormTable>

A few two-syllable adjectives also take -est: *quiet → the quietest, simple → the simplest*. Most others take **the most**.

<Example context="travel" fa="کابل بزرگ‌ترین شهر افغانستان است.">Kabul is <mark>the biggest</mark> city in Afghanistan.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این ارزان‌ترین موبایل دوکان است.">This is <mark>the cheapest</mark> phone in the store.</Example>
<Example context="news" fa="دیروز گرم‌ترین روز سال بود.">Yesterday was <mark>the hottest</mark> day of the year.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="دوشنبه شلوغ‌ترین روز هفته است.">Monday is <mark>the busiest</mark> day of the week.</Example>

## Long adjectives: the most

Long adjectives, and most two-syllable adjectives that don't end in -y, take **the most**.

<Example context="travel" fa="مزار شریف زیباترین شهری است که می‌شناسم.">Mazar-i-Sharif is <mark>the most beautiful</mark> city I know.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="ریاضی سخت‌ترین درس من است.">Math is <mark>the most difficult</mark> subject for me.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="این مهم‌ترین جلسه‌ی ماه است.">This is <mark>the most important</mark> meeting of the month.</Example>

Use **one** way: *the cheapest* or *the most expensive*, never ~~the most cheapest~~.

## Irregular: the best, the worst, the farthest

<FormTable caption="Irregular superlatives">

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| good | better | **the best** |
| bad | worse | **the worst** |
| far | farther / further | **the farthest / the furthest** |
| old (family) | older / elder | **the oldest / the eldest** |

</FormTable>

- **well** (= healthy) also changes: *well → better → best*.
- **eldest**, like *elder*, is for people in a family: *my **eldest** son*, *She's **the eldest** of four children.* **oldest** works for everything: *the **oldest** building in town*.

<Example context="social" fa="او بهترین دوست من است.">She's my <mark>best</mark> friend.</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="بدترین بخشِ سرماخوردگی شب‌هاست.">Nights are <mark>the worst</mark> part of a cold.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="پسر بزرگم دانشجوست.">My <mark>eldest</mark> son is a student.</Example>

## Don't forget the

A superlative almost always needs **the** before it: *the biggest, the most expensive, the best*. Persian «بزرگ‌ترین شهر» has no article, so learners often drop it. Say *Kabul is **the** biggest city*, not ~~Kabul is biggest city~~.

- A **possessive** can take the place of *the*: **my** best friend, **our** biggest problem, **Afghanistan's** highest mountain.
- You can stop after the superlative if the noun is clear: *"Which phone do you want?" "**The cheapest**."*

## in or of after a superlative?

<FormTable caption="in or of?">

| Use | After it | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **in** | a place or a group (singular) | the tallest building **in the world** · the youngest **in my class** · the oldest **in my family** |
| **of** | a plural, *all*, or a period of time | the youngest **of the three** · the best **of all** · the hottest day **of the year** · the best day **of my life** |

</FormTable>

Don't use *of* before a singular place or group: say *the best restaurant **in** the city*, not ~~of the city~~.

<Example context="news" fa="دریای خزر بزرگ‌ترین دریاچه‌ی جهان است.">The Caspian Sea is the largest lake <mark>in the world</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="من کوچک‌ترینِ سه برادرم.">I'm the youngest <mark>of the three</mark> brothers.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="امروز بهترین روز زندگی‌ام است!">Today is the best day <mark>of my life</mark>!</Example>

**one of the + superlative + plural noun** is very common: *It's **one of the best restaurants** in town.* The noun is plural (*restaurants*), because there are several "best" ones. More on this pattern in <LessonRef>B1 lesson 5 (Subject–verb agreement 1)</LessonRef>.

## the best … I've ever …

To say "in all my life until now", use the superlative + **present perfect** + **ever**.

<Example context="social" fa="این بهترین کبابی است که تا حالا خورده‌ام.">This is <mark>the best</mark> kebab <mark>I've ever eaten</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="این زیباترین جایی است که تا حالا دیده‌ام.">It's <mark>the most beautiful</mark> place <mark>I've ever seen</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="این سخت‌ترین کاری است که تا حالا انجام داده‌ام.">This is <mark>the hardest</mark> job <mark>I've ever done</mark>.</Example>

- The pattern: **the** + superlative + noun + **I've / you've / she's… ever** + past participle.
- **ever** can also come right after the noun: *It was **the hottest summer ever**.* With a comparative: *Prices are **higher than ever**.*
- The present perfect with **ever** is taught in <LessonRef>lesson 15 (Present perfect: experience (ever, never, been, gone))</LessonRef>.

## less and the least

**less** and **the least** are the opposite of *more* and *the most*. They show a **smaller** amount of a quality.

<FormTable caption="more / most and less / least">

| Up ↑ | Down ↓ |
|---|---|
| **more** expensive than | **less** expensive than |
| **the most** expensive | **the least** expensive |
| **the most** important | **the least** important |

</FormTable>

<Example context="shopping" fa="بس از هواپیما کم‌هزینه‌تر است.">The bus is <mark>less expensive</mark> than the plane.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="این کم‌خطرترین مسیر است.">This is <mark>the least dangerous</mark> road.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="کدام کار کمترین استرس را دارد؟">Which job is <mark>the least stressful</mark>?</Example>

- **less** and **the least** are most common with **long** adjectives: *less expensive, the least important*.
- With **short** adjectives, people often prefer **not as … as** or the opposite word: *He isn't as tall as me* or *He's shorter than me* sounds more natural than *He's less tall than me*. **not as … as** is in <LessonRef>lesson 39 (as … as, the same as, different from)</LessonRef>.
- **less** also goes with **uncountable nouns**: *less sugar, less time*. **less** or **fewer** with plural nouns is in <LessonRef>B1 lesson 45 (Comparatives 2: much better, more and more, the … the)</LessonRef>.

## Questions and negatives

<FormTable caption="Questions and negatives">

| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| What / Which / Who + be + the + superlative | **What's the biggest** city in your country? · **Who's the oldest** in your family? |
| Which + noun + is the + superlative? | **Which hotel is the cheapest**? |
| ever in a question | **What's the best film you've ever seen?** |
| Negative | It **isn't the cheapest** hotel, but it's **the cleanest**. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="بهترین کتابی که تا حالا خوانده‌ای چیست؟">What's <mark>the best</mark> book you've <mark>ever</mark> read?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="این ارزان‌ترین پرواز نیست، ولی سریع‌ترین است.">It <mark>isn't the cheapest</mark> flight, but it's <mark>the fastest</mark>.</Example>

**صفت عالی (superlative)** یک چیز را با **همه‌ی** اعضای یک گروه مقایسه می‌کند. در فارسی همه‌ی صفت‌ها با «ترین» عالی می‌شوند: ارزان‌ترین، گران‌ترین. در انگلیسی، مثل صفت تفضیلی (درس ۳۷)، دو راه داریم:

- **صفت کوتاه ← the + est-:** the cheapest (ارزان‌ترین)، the biggest (بزرگ‌ترین)، the nicest، the happiest. قواعد املایی مثل er- است: حرف آخر تکراری (big ← biggest) و y ← i (happy ← happiest).
- **صفت بلند ← the most:** the most expensive (گران‌ترین)، the most beautiful (زیباترین). هرگز هر دو با هم نه: ~~the most cheapest~~ غلط است.
- **بی‌قاعده‌ها:** the best (بهترین)، the worst (بدترین)، the farthest / furthest (دورترین). برای اعضای خانواده eldest هم داریم: my eldest son (پسر بزرگم). oldest برای همه چیز درست است.

**the را فراموش نکنید:** در فارسی می‌گوییم «کابل بزرگ‌ترین شهر افغانستان است» و هیچ حرف تعریفی نداریم. در انگلیسی صفت عالی تقریباً همیشه **the** می‌خواهد: Kabul is **the** biggest city in Afghanistan. فقط ضمیر ملکی می‌تواند جای the را بگیرد: my best friend (بهترین دوستم).

**in یا of؟** «در» و «از» فارسی را مستقیم ترجمه نکنید:

- **in** + مکان یا گروه (مفرد): the tallest building in the world (بلندترین ساختمانِ دنیا)، the youngest in my class. نگویید ~~of the world~~.
- **of** + جمع یا دوره‌ی زمانی: the youngest of the three (کوچک‌ترینِ آن سه نفر)، the best of all (بهترینِ همه)، the hottest day of the year (گرم‌ترین روزِ سال). «از همه» = of all، نه from all.
- **one of the + صفت عالی + اسم جمع:** one of the best restaurants (یکی از بهترین رستوران‌ها). اسم بعد از آن **جمع** است.

**بهترین … که تا حالا …:** فارسی و انگلیسی اینجا شبیه‌اند! «بهترین فیلمی که تا حالا دیده‌ام» = the best film **I've ever seen**. «تا حالا» = ever و فعل در زمان حال کامل (present perfect) است. با تفضیلی هم می‌شود: higher than ever (بالاتر از همیشه).

**less و the least:** برعکسِ more و the most هستند و مقدار **کمتر** یک ویژگی را نشان می‌دهند: less expensive (کم‌هزینه‌تر)، the least expensive (کم‌هزینه‌ترین)، the least important (کم‌اهمیت‌ترین). بیشتر با صفت‌های **بلند** به کار می‌روند. با صفت‌های کوتاه معمولاً not as … as یا صفت مخالف طبیعی‌تر است: He isn't as tall as me یا He's shorter than me (به‌جای less tall).

**سؤالی و منفی:** What's the biggest city in your country? (بزرگ‌ترین شهر کشورت کدام است؟) / What's the best film you've ever seen? (بهترین فیلمی که تا حالا دیده‌ای چیست؟) / It isn't the cheapest hotel, but it's the cleanest. (ارزان‌ترین هتل نیست، ولی تمیزترین است.)

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="Kabul is biggest city in Afghanistan." right="Kabul is the biggest city in Afghanistan." why="A superlative needs the (or a possessive like my)." />
<WrongRight wrong="It's the most cheapest hotel." right="It's the cheapest hotel." why="Use -est or the most, never both." />
<WrongRight wrong="She's the most good student in the class." right="She's the best student in the class." why="good is irregular: the best." />
<WrongRight wrong="It's the tallest building of the world." right="It's the tallest building in the world." why="in + a place or group, not of." />
<WrongRight wrong="He is the tallest from all." right="He is the tallest of all." why="Persian «از همه» = of all, not from all." />
<WrongRight wrong="It's one of the best restaurant in town." right="It's one of the best restaurants in town." why="one of the + superlative + plural noun." />
<WrongRight wrong="It's the best film I ever saw it." right="It's the best film I've ever seen." why="Don't repeat the object (it). The usual form is the present perfect: I've ever seen." />

<MemoryHook title="The top of the group">
A superlative is the **top** of a group, and the top always wears a hat: **the**. Then ask "where?": a place → **in** (in the world), a list or a time → **of** (of all, of the year). And "in all my life" → **I've ever**.
</MemoryHook>
