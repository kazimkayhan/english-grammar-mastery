## one and ones

When you don't want to repeat a **countable** noun, use **one** (singular) or **ones** (plural). The listener already knows the noun.

<Example context="shopping" fa="این پیراهن را دوست ندارم. آبیه را ترجیح می‌دهم.">I don't like this shirt. I prefer the <mark>blue one</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این کفش‌ها خیلی کوچک‌اند. بزرگ‌ترش را دارید؟">These shoes are too small. Do you have bigger <mark>ones</mark>?</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="گوشی‌ام خیلی قدیمی است. یکی نو لازم دارم.">My phone is very old. I need a <mark>new one</mark>.</Example>

<FormTable caption="Patterns with one / ones">

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| **the** + adjective + **one(s)** | the red **one** · the cheap **ones** |
| **a** + adjective + **one** | a new **one** · a bigger **one** |
| **this / that / which** + **one** | **this one** · **that one** · **Which one**? |
| **the one(s)** + place | the **one** on the left · the **ones** in the window |
| number / my + adjective + **one(s)** | two big **ones** · my old **one** |

</FormTable>

<Example context="travel" fa="«کدام چمدان مال توست؟» «آن سیاهه، سمت چپ.»">“Which suitcase is yours?” “The black <mark>one</mark> on the left.”</Example>

### No adjective? Watch the small words

- **a + noun → one**, with no *a*: *I need an umbrella. I'll buy **one**.* (never ~~a one~~)
- **some / any + plural noun → some / any**, usually without *ones*: *I need stamps. Do you have **any**?*
- After a **number** or a **possessive**, we don't add *one(s)* unless there is an adjective: *I'll take **two**. Take **mine**.* (but *two **big ones**, my **old one***). *mine, yours* are in <LessonRef>lesson 33 (mine, yours… and whose)</LessonRef>.
- **these / those** often stand alone: *I like **these**.* (*these ones* is also heard in informal speech.)

### Only for countable nouns

*one* replaces a noun you can count. With **uncountable** nouns, just leave the noun out: *Do you want hot milk or **cold**?* (not ~~cold one~~).

### one or it?

<FormTable caption="one or it?">

| You mean… | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **any** one of that kind (a / an + noun) | **one** | I lost my pen, so I bought **one**. |
| **that exact** thing (the / my / this + noun) | **it** | Then I found **it** under the sofa! |
| any of that kind (plural) | **some / any** (or adjective + **ones**) | I need new glasses. I'll get **some**. |
| those exact things (plural) | **them** | I lost my keys, but I found **them**. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="home" fa="«دوچرخه داری که قرض بدهی؟» «یکی دارم، ولی خیلی قدیمی است.»">“Do you have a bike I can borrow?” “I have <mark>one</mark>, but <mark>it</mark>'s very old.”</Example>

## another

**another** = *an + other*. It is **one word** (never ~~an other~~). It goes with a **singular** countable noun and has two meanings:

- **one more**: *Can I have **another** cup of tea?*
- **a different one**: *This restaurant is full. Let's go to **another** one.*

<Example context="social" fa="این کلوچه خوشمزه است. می‌شود یکی دیگر بردارم؟">These cookies are delicious. Can I have <mark>another</mark> one?</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="امروز وقت ندارم. بیا یک روز دیگر صحبت کنیم.">I'm busy today. Let's talk <mark>another</mark> day.</Example>

You can use **another** alone or with **one**: *Can I have **another**? / **another one**?*

With a **number** + plural noun, *another* means "extra": *We have to wait **another ten minutes**. She's staying **another two weeks**.*

Not with a plural noun alone: ~~another cookies~~. Say ***more** cookies* or ***other** cookies*.

## other and others

**other** goes **before a plural** (or uncountable) noun. It never takes **-s** there.

<Example context="travel" fa="آدم‌های دیگری در اتاق انتظار هستند.">There are <mark>other</mark> people in the waiting room.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این رنگ‌های دیگر را هم دارید؟">Do you have this jacket in <mark>other</mark> colors?</Example>

**others** stands **alone**: it means *other people* or *other things*, with no noun after it.

<Example context="school" fa="بعضی از دانشجوها پیاده می‌آیند؛ بقیه با اتوبوس.">Some students walk to school. <mark>Others</mark> take the bus.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="با دیگران مهربان باش.">Be kind to <mark>others</mark>.</Example>

So: **other** students, but **others** (alone). Never ~~others students~~.

With a **singular** noun, *other* needs a word before it: *the other book, my other sister, any other questions?* Without one, use **another**: *another book*.

## the other and the others

Add **the** when you mean a **particular** one or group.

- **the other** = the **second of two**: *I have two brothers. One lives in Kabul, and **the other** lives in Paris.*
- **the others** = **the rest** of a group: *Zahra is here. **The others** are coming later.*

<Example context="home" fa="یک لنگه جوراب این‌جاست. آن یکی کجاست؟">One sock is here. Where's <mark>the other</mark> one?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="بانک آن طرف خیابان است.">The bank is on <mark>the other</mark> side of the street.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="احمد این‌جاست، ولی بقیه هنوز در راه‌اند.">Ahmad is here, but <mark>the others</mark> are still on the way.</Example>

<FormTable caption="another, other, the other: a summary">

| Word | Meaning | + noun | Alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **another** | one more / a different one | another cup | another (one) |
| **other** | different (plural) | other people | **others** |
| **the other** | the second of two | the other shoe | the other (one) |
| **the other(s)** | the rest of a group | the other students | **the others** |

</FormTable>

**the other day** is a fixed phrase. It means "a few days ago": *I saw Karim **the other day**.*

*each other* (*They looked at each other*) is in <LessonRef>B1 lesson 43 (Generic you, one, they; each other)</LessonRef>.

<MemoryHook title="An-other, the other">
**another** = **an** + other, so it is singular, like *an*: *another cup*. **the other** has **the**, so it is a particular one: the last one of two. Add **-s** only when **no noun** follows: *others*, *the others*.
</MemoryHook>
