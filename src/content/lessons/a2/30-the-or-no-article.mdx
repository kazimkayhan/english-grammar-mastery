## In general or specific?

You met **the** in <LessonRef>A1 lesson 10, *the: first steps*</LessonRef>: *the* means *you know which one*. Now we look at the opposite: when do we use **no article**?

With **plural** nouns (*dogs, books*) and **uncountable** nouns (*music, water, life*), there are two meanings:

<FormTable caption="General or specific?">

| Meaning | Article | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **in general** (all of them, the idea) | no article | I love **music**. · **Dogs** are friendly. |
| **specific** (we know which ones) | **the** | I love **the** music **in this film**. · **The** dogs **next door** are noisy. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="من موسیقی را خیلی دوست دارم.">I really love <mark>music</mark>. (all music)</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="موسیقی این فیلم فوق‌العاده است."><mark>The music</mark> in this film is amazing. (this film's music)</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="سگ‌ها حیوانات باوفایی هستند."><mark>Dogs</mark> are loyal animals. (all dogs)</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="سگ‌های همسایه تمام شب پارس کردند."><mark>The dogs</mark> next door barked all night. (those dogs)</Example>

### More examples in general

<Example context="health" fa="سبزیجات برای سلامتی خوب هستند."><mark>Vegetables</mark> are good for your health.</Example>
<Example context="news" fa="زندگی در شهرهای بزرگ گران است."><mark>Life</mark> in big cities is expensive.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="قهوه این روزها گران است."><mark>Coffee</mark> is expensive these days.</Example>

### What makes it specific?

Usually something tells us **which ones**: words after the noun (*in this film, next door, on the table*), the situation, or an earlier mention.

<Example context="shopping" fa="قهوه‌ی این کافه عالی است."><mark>The coffee</mark> at this café is excellent.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="سبزی‌هایی که دیروز خریدی کجاست؟">Where are <mark>the vegetables</mark> you bought yesterday?</Example>

Compare *some* too. **some** = an amount; **no article** = in general. *I bought **some** bread* (an amount). *I don't eat **bread*** (bread in general). (some and any: <LessonRef>A1 lesson 15, *Countable & uncountable nouns; some, any, how much, how many*</LessonRef>.)

And remember: a **singular countable** noun can't be alone. *I love **dogs*** (general, plural) or *I have **a** dog*, but never ~~I love dog~~. (Other ways to talk in general, like *The tiger is…*, come in <LessonRef>B2 lesson 32, *Articles 3: generic and abstract*</LessonRef>.)

## Set phrases with no article

Some common phrases have **no article**. Learn them as fixed groups.

<FormTable caption="Set phrases with no article">

| Phrase | Example |
|---|---|
| **at home** · go **home** | I stayed **at home** yesterday. · Let's go **home**. |
| go to **bed** · in **bed** | I go **to bed** at eleven. · She's still **in bed**. |
| go to **work** · at **work** | He goes **to work** at 8. · She's **at work** now. |
| go to **school** · go to **college** (as a student) | My son goes **to school** by bus. |
| by **bus** / **car** / **train** / **plane** · on **foot** | I go to work **by bus**. · We came **on foot**. |
| watch **TV** | We watched **TV** after dinner. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="home" fa="دیشب زود به رختخواب رفتم.">I went to <mark>bed</mark> early last night.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="نمی‌توانم صحبت کنم؛ سر کارم.">I can't talk now. I'm at <mark>work</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="با بس به قندهار رفتیم.">We went to Kandahar by <mark>bus</mark>.</Example>

- **go home** has **no to**: *Let's go home*, not ~~go to home~~ (<LessonRef>A2 lesson 44, *Prepositions of movement*</LessonRef>).
- **by + transport** has no article. With *my, the* or *a*, use another preposition: *by car*, but *in my car*; *by bus*, but *on the bus*.
- **TV and the radio are different:** *watch **TV***, but *listen to **the radio***, *on **the radio***.
- *go to school* (as a student) vs *go to **the** school* (the building, for example for a meeting): that and *hospital, prison, church* are in <LessonRef>B1 lesson 37, *Articles 2: places, institutions, the rich*</LessonRef>.

## Meals, days and months

### Meals

**breakfast, lunch, dinner** usually have **no article**: *have breakfast, for lunch, after dinner*.

<Example context="home" fa="ساعت هفت صبحانه می‌خورم.">I have <mark>breakfast</mark> at seven.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="برای ناهار چه داریم؟">What's for <mark>lunch</mark>?</Example>

But use **a** when there is an **adjective**, and **the** for a specific meal:

<Example context="travel" fa="یک صبحانه‌ی مفصل خوردیم.">We had <mark>a big breakfast</mark> at the hotel.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="شامی که در خانه‌ی شما خوردیم عالی بود."><mark>The dinner</mark> we had at your house was wonderful.</Example>

### Days, months and seasons

Days and months have **no article**: *on **Monday**, in **July**, on **Friday** evening*. Seasons can be with or without **the**: *in **summer*** or *in **the** summer*.

<Example context="work" fa="جلسه دوشنبه است.">The meeting is on <mark>Monday</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="تولدم در ماه ژوئیه است.">My birthday is in <mark>July</mark>.</Example>

*next* and *last* also have no article: *next week, last year* (<LessonRef>A1 lesson 28, *Past time phrases: yesterday, last week, ago*</LessonRef>).

## Sports, languages and school subjects

These also have **no article**.

<FormTable caption="No article">

| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| sports and games | play **football**, **tennis**, **volleyball**, **chess** |
| languages | speak **English**, learn **Persian**, **French** is difficult |
| school subjects | study **history**, **math**, **chemistry** |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="آخر هفته‌ها فوتبال بازی می‌کنیم.">We play <mark>football</mark> on weekends.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="او انگلیسی و فرانسوی صحبت می‌کند.">She speaks <mark>English</mark> and <mark>French</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="خواهرم در پوهنتون شیمی می‌خواند.">My sister studies <mark>chemistry</mark> in college.</Example>

<UkNote>American English says **math**; British English says **maths**. Americans usually say *go to college* / *in college*; British speakers say *go to university* / *at university* (also no article). In American English, *football* often means American football; the world game is **soccer**.</UkNote>

**Watch out:** musical instruments usually take **the**: *play **the** piano, play **the** guitar*. That is in <LessonRef>B1 lesson 37, *Articles 2: places, institutions, the rich*</LessonRef>.

<Persian>

**the یا بدون حرف تعریف؟**

**کلی یا مشخص:** با اسم‌های جمع و غیرقابل‌شمارش:

- وقتی **به طور کلی** حرف می‌زنیم ← بدون the: I love music. (موسیقی دوست دارم، هر نوع موسیقی.) / Dogs are friendly. (سگ‌ها مهربان‌اند.) / Life is beautiful. (زندگی زیباست.)
- وقتی منظورمان یک چیز **مشخص** است ← the: I love the music in this film. (موسیقی این فیلم را دوست دارم.) / The dogs next door are noisy. (سگ‌های همسایه پر سر و صدا هستند.)

**چرا فارسی‌زبان‌ها the اضافه می‌کنند؟** در فارسی برای مفعول، حتی وقتی کلی حرف می‌زنیم، «را» می‌آوریم: «موسیقی **را** دوست دارم». چون «را» معمولاً با the ترجمه می‌شود، فارسی‌زبان‌ها می‌گویند I love the music ✗. اما در انگلیسی برای معنای کلی the نمی‌آید: I love music ✓. همین‌طور The life is beautiful ✗ ← Life is beautiful ✓.

**عبارت‌های ثابت بدون حرف تعریف:**

- at home (در خانه)، go home (رفتن به خانه، بدون to)
- go to bed (به رختخواب رفتن)، in bed (در رختخواب)
- go to work (سر کار رفتن)، at work (سر کار)
- go to school (مدرسه رفتن، به عنوان دانش‌آموز)
- by bus / by car / by train (با اتوبوس / ماشین / قطار)، on foot (پیاده)
- watch TV (تلویزیون دیدن)؛ اما listen to **the** radio (رادیو گوش دادن، با the)

**وعده‌های غذایی:** have breakfast / lunch / dinner بدون the: I had breakfast (صبحانه خوردم)، نه I had the breakfast. اگر صفت بیاید ← a: a big breakfast. اگر وعده‌ی مشخصی باشد ← the: The dinner at your house was great.

**روزها و ماه‌ها:** on Monday، in July (بدون the). فصل‌ها با the یا بدون آن: in summer / in the summer.

**ورزش، زبان و درس:** play football، speak English، study history؛ همه بدون the. اما سازهای موسیقی معمولاً با the: play the piano.

</Persian>

<MemoryHook title="Which ones? → the">
Before you say **the**, ask: *“Which ones?”* If you can answer (*the ones in this film, the ones next door*), say **the**. If the answer is *all of them, in general*, say **nothing**: *I love music. Dogs are friendly.*
</MemoryHook>
