## as … as: equal

Put an **adjective** or an **adverb** between two **as**. It means the two things are **equal**.

<FormTable caption="as … as">

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| as + **adjective** + as | Karim is **as tall as** Ahmad. |
| as + **adverb** + as | She speaks **as fast as** her mother. |
| as + adjective + as + **pronoun** | He's **as old as me**. (everyday) / **as old as I am**. (careful) |
| as + adjective + as + **clause** | The test was **as easy as I thought**. |

</FormTable>

- Use the **base form** of the adjective: *as **big** as*, never ~~as bigger as~~.
- After the second **as**, pronouns work like after *than* (<LessonRef>lesson 37 (Comparatives)</LessonRef>): **as tall as me** in everyday English, **as tall as I am** in careful English. For other verbs, use *do*: *She works as hard **as I do**.*
- With a **verb**, use an **adverb**: *He drives as **carefully** as his wife* (not ~~as careful~~). Adverbs are in <LessonRef>lesson 41 (Adverbs of manner: quickly, well)</LessonRef>.

<Example context="home" fa="کریم حالا هم‌قدِ احمد است.">Karim is <mark>as tall as</mark> Ahmad now.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این گوشی به اندازه‌ی آن یکی خوب است، ولی ارزان‌تر است.">This phone is <mark>as good as</mark> that one, but it's cheaper.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="او به اندازه‌ی من سخت کار می‌کند.">She works <mark>as hard as</mark> I do.</Example>

## not as … as: less

**not as … as** means the first thing is **less**. *The bus isn't as fast as the train* = the train is faster.

<Example context="travel" fa="اتوبوس به سرعتِ قطار نیست.">The bus <mark>isn't as fast as</mark> the train.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="امتحان به سختیِ آنچه فکر می‌کردم نبود.">The exam <mark>wasn't as difficult as</mark> I thought.</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="سردردم به بدیِ دیروز نیست.">My headache <mark>isn't as bad as</mark> yesterday.</Example>

- **not so … as** is also correct: *The bus isn't **so** fast **as** the train.* **not as … as** is more common.
- **not as … as** can sound gentler than a comparative: *The food wasn't as good as last time* is softer than *The food was worse than last time*.
- With **short** adjectives, **not as … as** is usually better than **less**: say *He isn't as tall as me*, not *He's less tall than me*. (**less** is in <LessonRef>lesson 38 (Superlatives)</LessonRef>.)

## as much / as many + noun + as

To compare **amounts**, put a noun after *as much* or *as many*:

- **as much** + uncountable noun: *I don't have **as much free time as** you.*
- **as many** + plural noun: *She has **as many books as** a library.*
- With no noun: *I eat **as much as** my brother.*

*much* and *many* are in <LessonRef>lesson 31 (much, many, a lot of, a few, a little)</LessonRef>.

<Example context="work" fa="امسال به اندازه‌ی پارسال مشتری نداریم.">We don't have <mark>as many customers as</mark> last year.</Example>

## Questions with as … as

<Example context="shopping" fa="گوشی جدید به خوبیِ قبلی است؟">Is the new phone <mark>as good as</mark> the old one?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="قندهار به اندازه‌ی کابل شلوغ است؟">Is Kandahar <mark>as busy as</mark> Kabul?</Example>

## the same as

**the same** means *not different*. It always has **the**.

<FormTable caption="the same">

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| A is **the same as** B | My phone is **the same as** yours. |
| **the same** + noun + **as** | She's **the same age as** me. · It's **the same color as** my car. |
| A and B are **the same** | Our phones are **the same**. |
| **the same** + noun | We go to **the same** school. |

</FormTable>

- Always **the**: *the same*, never ~~same as~~ or ~~a same~~.
- Use **as**, not *like* or *with*: ~~the same like~~, ~~the same with~~.
- Negative: *Your problem **isn't the same as** mine.* Question: *Are you **the same age as** Ahmad?*

<Example context="school" fa="من و زهرا هم‌سن هستیم.">Zahra is <mark>the same age as</mark> me.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="حقوق من با حقوق او یکی است.">My salary is <mark>the same as</mark> hers.</Example>

## similar to, different from

<FormTable caption="similar and different">

| Word | Preposition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **similar** (almost the same) | **to** | Her dress is **similar to** mine. |
| **different** (not the same) | **from** | Life in Canada is **different from** life in Afghanistan. |

</FormTable>

- **similar to**, not ~~similar with~~.
- **different from** is correct everywhere. Americans also say **different than**. Never ~~different with~~ or ~~different of~~.
- With two things as the subject, you can stop after the adjective: *Our houses are **similar**. The two cities are very **different**.*
- Questions: *How **is** Kabul **different from** Mazar-i-Sharif? What's the **difference between** them?*

<Example context="travel" fa="غذای اینجا شبیه غذای افغان است.">The food here is <mark>similar to</mark> Afghan food.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="او با برادرش خیلی فرق دارد.">She's very <mark>different from</mark> her brother.</Example>

<UkNote>In spoken British English, people also say **different to**: *His job is different to mine.*</UkNote>

To say one thing *looks* or *sounds* like another (*She looks like her mother*), see <LessonRef>lesson 17 (Linking verbs: look, feel, sound, seem + adjective)</LessonRef>. **like** or **as** (*like a teacher / as a teacher*) is in <LessonRef>B2 lesson 39 (Comparison 3: twice as…, by far, like or as)</LessonRef>.

## as soon as possible, as much as you can

**as … as possible** and **as … as you can** mean *the most you are able to*.

<FormTable caption="as … as possible / as … as you can">

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **as soon as possible** | very soon, with no delay | Please reply **as soon as possible**. |
| **as soon as you can** | the same | Call me **as soon as you can**. |
| **as much as you can** | the biggest amount you are able to | Drink **as much** water **as you can**. |
| **as quickly / early / often as possible** | quickest, earliest, most often | Come **as early as possible**. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="work" fa="لطفاً هرچه زودتر جواب بدهید.">Please answer <mark>as soon as possible</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="تا جایی که می‌توانی آب بخور.">Drink <mark>as much</mark> water <mark>as you can</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="تا می‌توانی زیاد تمرین کن.">Practice <mark>as often as possible</mark>.</Example>

- In emails and messages, people write **ASAP**: *Send it ASAP.*
- **as soon as** + a clause (*Call me **as soon as** you get home*) is a time word, not a comparison. That is in <LessonRef>lesson 27 (First conditional & future time clauses)</LessonRef>.

<Persian>

**as … as = به اندازه‌ی / هم‌…:** وقتی دو چیز **برابرند**، صفت یا قید را بین دو as می‌گذاریم: Karim is as tall as Ahmad. (کریم هم‌قدِ احمد است / کریم به اندازه‌ی احمد قد بلند است.) صفت به شکل **ساده** می‌آید، نه تفضیلی: as big as، نه as bigger as. با فعل، از قید استفاده کنید: He drives as carefully as his wife.

**not as … as = کمتر:** The bus isn't as fast as the train. (اتوبوس به سرعتِ قطار نیست = قطار سریع‌تر است.) not so … as هم درست است. این ساختار گاهی ملایم‌تر از تفضیلی است. با صفت‌های کوتاه، not as … as از less طبیعی‌تر است.

**ضمیر بعد از as:** مثل than: در گفتار as tall as me، در نوشتار دقیق as tall as I am. برای فعل‌های دیگر: as hard as I do.

**as much / as many:** برای مقدار: as much + اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش (as much time as)، as many + اسم جمع (as many books as).

**the same as = مثلِ / همان:** همیشه با **the**: My phone is the same as yours. (گوشی من مثل مال توست.) در فارسی حرف تعریف نداریم، برای همین فارسی‌زبان‌ها the را جا می‌اندازند. «مثلِ» را هم like ترجمه می‌کنند و می‌گویند the same like که غلط است. the same + اسم + as: the same age as me (هم‌سنِ من).

**similar to و different from:** حرف اضافه‌ی فارسی را ترجمه نکنید. در فارسی می‌گوییم «**با** او فرق دارد»، برای همین می‌گویند different with، که غلط است. بگویید **different from** (در آمریکا different than هم می‌گویند). «شبیهِ» = **similar to**، نه similar with.

**as soon as possible = هرچه زودتر:** در فارسی برای «تا جایی که ممکن است» از تفضیلی استفاده می‌کنیم: «هرچه زودتر»، «هرچه بیشتر». در انگلیسی از as … as استفاده می‌کنیم: as soon as possible، as much as you can (هرچه بیشتر که می‌توانی)، as early as possible (هرچه زودتر / تا حد ممکن زود). در پیام‌ها: ASAP.

**سؤالی و منفی:** Is the new phone as good as the old one? (گوشی جدید به خوبیِ قبلی است؟) / Your problem isn't the same as mine. (مشکل تو مثل مشکل من نیست.) / How is Kabul different from Mazar-i-Sharif? (کابل چه فرقی با مزار شریف دارد؟)

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="Karim is as taller as Ahmad." right="Karim is as tall as Ahmad." why="Use the base form between the two as." />
<WrongRight wrong="The bus isn't as fast than the train." right="The bus isn't as fast as the train." why="as … as, not as … than." />
<WrongRight wrong="My phone is same like yours." right="My phone is the same as yours." why="the same as: always the, and as (not like)." />
<WrongRight wrong="Her life is very different with mine." right="Her life is very different from mine." why="different from (US also than). Persian «با … فرق دارد» → not with." />
<WrongRight wrong="This song is similar with that one." right="This song is similar to that one." why="similar to, not similar with." />
<WrongRight wrong="Please answer sooner as possible." right="Please answer as soon as possible." why="Persian «هرچه زودتر» is a comparative, but English uses as … as possible." />

<MemoryHook title="Two as, one middle">
Think of **as … as** as a balance: one thing on each side, the plain adjective in the middle, and the scale is **level**. Add **not**, and the first side goes **down**. For pairs of words, learn the fixed partner: **same as**, **similar to**, **different from**.
</MemoryHook>
