## The forms

<FormTable caption="be: present simple">

| Subject | Positive | Short form | Negative | Short negative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | I **am** | I'm | I am not | I'm not |
| he / she / it | she **is** | she's | she is not | she isn't / she's not |
| you / we / they | they **are** | they're | they are not | they aren't / they're not |

</FormTable>

Three things to remember:

- **You** always takes **are**, even when you talk to one person: *You are my best friend.*
- **We** and **they** take **are**. So do *you* and any plural noun (see below).
- The **full** and **short** forms mean the same thing. In speech we usually use the short forms (*I'm, she's, they're*). Full forms sound slower or more formal, and we use them when we want to stress a word: *I am not a liar!*

## Singular or plural?

One person or thing takes **is**. Two or more take **are**.

<Example context="home" fa="برادرم معلم است.">My brother <mark>is</mark> a teacher.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="برادرهایم معلم هستند.">My brothers <mark>are</mark> teachers.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="ماشین جدید است.">The car <mark>is</mark> new.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="ماشین‌ها جدید هستند.">The cars <mark>are</mark> new.</Example>

Many learners say *"My brothers is teachers."* Say **are** with every plural noun.

## Short forms

<Example context="work" fa="من پرستار هستم."><mark>I'm</mark> a nurse.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="او ۲۰ ساله است."><mark>She's</mark> 20 years old.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="بچه‌ها خانه هستند.">The children <mark>are</mark> at home.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="ما افغان هستیم."><mark>We're</mark> Afghan. <mark>We're</mark> from Mazar-i-Sharif.</Example>

Watch the **'s**. It can mean three things, and context tells you which:

- **'s = is**: *She's a nurse.* (she is)
- **'s = has**: *She's got a car.* (she has, <LessonRef>lesson 16</LessonRef>)
- **'s = possessive**: *Zahra's car.* (Zahra's, <LessonRef>lesson 12</LessonRef>)

## Negatives

Put **not** after the verb. For a short negative, join the verb and *not* with an apostrophe.

<FormTable caption="be: negative">

| Full negative | Short negative |
|---|---|
| I am not | **I'm not** (the only short form for *I*) |
| he / she / it is not | he / she / it **isn't** or **'s not** |
| you / we / they are not | you / we / they **aren't** or **'re not** |

</FormTable>

**There is no short form “amn't”.** Standard English does not contract *am not*, so the short form for *I* is always **I'm not**. (In questions, people say *Aren't I…?* instead. That is <LessonRef>lesson 6</LessonRef>.)

<Example context="health" fa="خسته نیستم، فقط گرسنه‌ام."><mark>I'm not</mark> tired. <mark>I'm</mark> just hungry.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این کفش‌ها گران نیستند.">These shoes <mark>aren't</mark> expensive.</Example>

## Questions with be

To ask a yes/no question, **swap** the subject and the verb: *You are ready* → *Are you ready?* With **be** we never add *do*.

<FormTable caption="Questions with be">

| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| **Am** I …? | Am I late? |
| **Is** he / she / it …? | Is she at home? |
| **Are** you / we / they …? | Are they students? |
| Question word + **be** + subject | **Where is** she? · **What are** they? |

</FormTable>

<Example context="work" fa="شما پرستار هستید؟">Are <mark>you</mark> a nurse?</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="او خانه است؟">Is <mark>she</mark> at home?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="کجا هستی؟">Where <mark>are</mark> you?</Example>

Short answers (*Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.*) and more practice come in <LessonRef>lesson 6</LessonRef>.

## Uses of be

Use **be** to say who or what something is, how old, where, how someone feels, and what something is like.

- **Name and job**: *I'm Zahra. I'm a teacher.*
- **Nationality and origin**: *She's Canadian. She's from Toronto.*
- **Age**: *He's 35.* Say the age with **be**, never with *have*: **I'm 20**, not ~~I have 20 years~~. Both *I'm 20* and *I'm 20 years old* are correct.
- **Feelings and states**: *We're happy. They're cold.*
- **Place**: *They're at home. The keys are on the table.*
- **Description** (with an adjective): *The house is big. It isn't new.*

<Example context="social" fa="او ۳۵ ساله است.">He's <mark>35</mark>. (be, not have)</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="هتل ما نزدیک ایستگاه است.">Our hotel <mark>is</mark> near the station.</Example>

## Be is also a helper

In this lesson **be** is the main verb. *Am, is* and *are* also work as helpers with **-ing** verbs: *I'm working.* That is the present continuous, in <LessonRef>lesson 22</LessonRef>. You don't need it yet.

<Persian>

**فعل be (بودن) در زمان حال ساده سه شکل دارد:**

- با **I** (من) ← **am**: I am a student. (من دانشجو هستم.)
- با **he / she / it** (او / آن) ← **is**: She is a doctor. (او پزشک است.)
- با **you / we / they** (تو، شما، ما، آن‌ها) ← **are**: We are at home. (ما خانه هستیم.)

**جمع:** هر اسم جمع (بیش از یکی) با **are** می‌آید: My brothers are teachers. (برادرهایم معلم هستند.) نه My brothers is teachers.

**you** همیشه با **are** می‌آید، حتی وقتی با یک نفر صحبت می‌کنیم: You are kind. (تو مهربانی.)

**مهم‌ترین نکته برای فارسی‌زبان‌ها:** در فارسی گاهی فعل را به کلمه می‌چسبانیم («خسته‌ام») یا اصلاً نمی‌گوییم. اما در انگلیسی **هر جمله باید فعل داشته باشد**. پس باید بگوییم I am tired، نه I tired.

**حالت کوتاه (short form):** I'm، he's، she's، it's، you're، we're، they're. در گفتار معمولاً از حالت کوتاه استفاده می‌کنیم. دقت کنید: «'s» می‌تواند is (او هست) یا has یا نشان‌دهنده‌ی مالکیت باشد؛ از جمله معنی‌اش را تشخیص می‌دهیم.

**منفی:** not را بعد از am / is / are می‌گذاریم: I'm not، she isn't، they aren't. برای I فقط **I'm not** داریم. «amn't» در انگلیسی معیار وجود ندارد.

**سن:** در فارسی می‌گوییم «بیست سال دارم»، اما در انگلیسی از be استفاده می‌کنیم: I'm 20. (نه I have 20 years.)

**نام، شغل، ملیت، احساس، مکان و توصیف** هم با be می‌آیند: I'm Zahra. / I'm a nurse. / We're Afghan. / She's tired. / They're at home. / The house is big.

**سؤالی:** فعل be با فاعل جابه‌جا می‌شود: You are ready ← Are you ready? (آیا تو آماده‌ای؟) برای be از do استفاده نمی‌کنیم: نه Do you are… . جمله‌های پرسشی با کلمه‌ی پرسشی: Where is she؟ (او کجاست؟) · What are they؟ (آن‌ها چه هستند؟)

</Persian>

<MemoryHook title="One, one, many">
**I** → **am**. **One** person or thing (he, she, it, a name, a job) → **is**. **You**, **we**, **they** and anything in a group (plural) → **are**.
