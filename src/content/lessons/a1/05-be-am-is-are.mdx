## The forms

<FormTable caption="be: present simple">

| Subject | Positive | Short form | Negative | Short negative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | I **am** | I'm | I am not | I'm not |
| he / she / it | she **is** | she's | she is not | she isn't / she's not |
| you / we / they | they **are** | they're | they are not | they aren't / they're not |

</FormTable>

Notice: **you** always takes **are**, even when you talk to one person: *You are my best friend.*

In speech and friendly writing we almost always use the **short forms** (*I'm, she's, they're*). In very formal writing, use the full forms.

## When do we use be?

Use **be** to say who or what something is, and to describe it.

<Example context="work" fa="من پرستار هستم."><mark>I'm</mark> a nurse.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="او ۲۰ ساله است."><mark>She's</mark> 20 years old.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="بچه‌ها خانه هستند.">The children <mark>are</mark> at home.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="ما ایرانی هستیم."><mark>We're</mark> Iranian. <mark>We're</mark> from Isfahan.</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="خسته نیستم، فقط گرسنه‌ام."><mark>I'm not</mark> tired. <mark>I'm</mark> just hungry.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این کفش‌ها گران نیستند.">These shoes <mark>aren't</mark> expensive.</Example>

So **be** is used for:

- **name and job**: *I'm Sara. I'm a teacher.*
- **age**: *He's 35.*
- **feelings and states**: *We're happy. They're cold.*
- **place**: *The keys are on the table.*
- **nationality and origin**: *She's Canadian. She's from Toronto.*
- **descriptions**: *The house is big. It isn't new.*

## Questions come in the next lesson

To make a question, we simply swap the subject and the verb: *You are ready* → ***Are you** ready?* You'll practise this in lesson A1.6.

<Persian>

**فعل be (بودن) در زمان حال سه شکل دارد:**

- با **I** (من) ← **am**: ‏I am a student. (من دانشجو هستم.)
- با **he / she / it** (او / آن) ← **is**: ‏She is a doctor. (او پزشک است.)
- با **you / we / they** (تو، شما، ما، آن‌ها) ← **are**: ‏They are at home. (آن‌ها خانه هستند.)

**مهم‌ترین نکته برای فارسی‌زبان‌ها:** در فارسی گاهی فعل را به کلمه می‌چسبانیم («خسته‌ام») یا اصلاً نمی‌گوییم. اما در انگلیسی **هر جمله باید فعل داشته باشد**. پس باید بگوییم I am tired، نه I tired.

**سن:** در فارسی می‌گوییم «بیست سال دارم»، ولی در انگلیسی از be استفاده می‌کنیم: I'm 20. (نه I have 20 years.)

**منفی:** کلمه‌ی not را بعد از am / is / are می‌گذاریم: I'm not ، she isn't ، they aren't.

</Persian>

**I** am alone → **am**. **One** other person or thing → **is**. **You** and everyone in a group → **are**.
