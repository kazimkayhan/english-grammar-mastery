## Possessive words

<FormTable caption="Subject → possessive">

| Subject | Possessive | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | **my** | This is **my** bag. |
| you | **your** | Is this **your** phone? |
| he | **his** | Reza and **his** wife |
| she | **her** | Maryam and **her** husband |
| it | **its** | The cat and **its** toy |
| we | **our** | **Our** house is small. |
| they | **their** | **Their** children are at school. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="این برادرم و همسرش هستند.">This is my brother and <mark>his</mark> wife.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="این خانم مدیر ماست. دفترش طبقه‌ی دوم است.">This is <mark>our</mark> manager. <mark>Her</mark> office is on the second floor.</Example>

## his or her? Look at the owner

The choice depends on the **owner**, not on the thing: *Reza and **his** mother* (Reza is a man) · *Maryam and **her** husband* (Maryam is a woman).

## 's for people

To say something belongs to a person, add **'s** to the person. The **owner comes first**:

<Example context="home" fa="این گوشیِ سارا است.">This is <mark>Sara's</mark> phone.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="ماشینِ پدرم در پارکینگ است."><mark>My father's</mark> car is in the parking lot.</Example>

## its or it's?

- **its** = belonging to *it*: *The company changed **its** name.*
- **it's** = *it is*: ***It's** a good company.*

<Persian>

**صفت‌های ملکی:** my (ـَم / مال من)، your (ـَت / مال تو)، his (مال او، مرد)، her (مال او، زن)، its (مال آن)، our (ـِمان)، their (ـِشان).

- کتابم = my book، خانه‌مان = our house

**his یا her؟** به «صاحب» نگاه کنید، نه به چیز: «مریم و شوهرش» = Maryam and **her** husband (چون مریم زن است).

**'s برای آدم‌ها:** «ماشینِ سارا» در انگلیسی برعکس است: **Sara's car**. اول صاحب، بعد s'، بعد چیز.

**its و it's:** its یعنی «مالِ آن» و آپاستروف ندارد. it's یعنی it is.

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="My sister lives with his husband." right="My sister lives with her husband." why="The owner is a woman → her." />
<WrongRight wrong="the book of my teacher" right="my teacher's book" why="For people, use 's: owner first." />

<MemoryHook title="Owner first, then the thing">
English puts the **owner first**: **Sara's** car, **my** car. Persian puts it last (ماشینِ **سارا**). Flip it!
</MemoryHook>
