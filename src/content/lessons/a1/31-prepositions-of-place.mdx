## in, on, at

<FormTable caption="in, on, at for place">

| | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **in** | inside a space with limits | in the box · in the kitchen · in my bag · in Tehran · in Iran |
| **on** | on a surface or a line | on the table · on the wall · on the floor · on the second floor |
| **at** | at a point, or a place for an activity | at the door · at the bus stop · at work · at school · at home |

</FormTable>

## Addresses: in → on → at

Zoom in on the map: the city, then the street, then the house.

<FormTable caption="Prepositions in addresses">

| | Example |
|---|---|
| **in** + city / country | I live **in** Tehran. |
| **on** + street | I live **on** Azadi Street. |
| **at** + number + street | I live **at** 12 Azadi Street. |

</FormTable>

<UkNote>British speakers often say **in** a street (*She lives in Oxford Street*) and use **opposite** where Americans also say **across from**.</UkNote>

<Persian>

**«در» در فارسی، سه کلمه در انگلیسی:**

- **in** = داخلِ یک فضای بسته یا محدود: in the kitchen، in Tehran
- **on** = روی یک سطح: on the table، on the wall
- **at** = در یک نقطه یا محلی برای یک فعالیت: at the door، at work، at school

**وسیله‌ی نقلیه:** اگر بتوانید داخلش راه بروید ← on (on the bus، on the plane). اگر نه ← in (in the car، in a taxi).

**کلمه‌های دیگر:**

- under = زیر
- next to = کنار
- between … and … = بین … و …
- behind = پشت
- in front of = جلوی
- near = نزدیکِ
- opposite = روبه‌روی

دقت کنید: «بین بانک **و** نانوایی» = between the bank **and** the bakery (نه with). «کنارِ» = next **to** (نه next of). «روبه‌روی» = opposite (بدون to).

**آدرس:** شهر با in، خیابان با on، پلاک با at.

</Persian>
