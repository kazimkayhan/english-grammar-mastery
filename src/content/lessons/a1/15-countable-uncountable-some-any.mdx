## Can you count it?

Ask one question: can you say **one ___, two ___s**?

- **Yes** → the noun is **countable**: *one egg, two eggs · one chair, three chairs*.
- **No** → the noun is **uncountable**: *milk, water, rice, money, bread, sugar, cheese, meat, music, time*. You can't say ~~two milks~~ or ~~three rices~~.

<FormTable caption="Countable and uncountable">

| | Countable | Uncountable |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | egg, apple, chair, book, friend | milk, water, rice, money, bread, sugar |
| a / an? | **an** egg | no: ~~a milk~~ |
| plural -s? | two egg**s** | no: ~~milks~~ |
| verb | The eggs **are** fresh. | The milk **is** fresh. |
| some (+) | some egg**s** | some milk |
| any (− ?) | any eggs? | any milk? |
| How…? | How **many** eggs? | How **much** milk? |
| a lot of | a lot of eggs | a lot of milk |

</FormTable>

## Uncountable nouns

An uncountable noun has **no a / an** and **no plural**. It takes a **singular** verb (*is*, not *are*).

<Example context="home" fa="برنج آماده است.">The rice <mark>is</mark> ready.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="پول همه چیز نیست."><mark>Money isn't</mark> everything.</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="آب برای سلامتی خوب است."><mark>Water is</mark> good for your health.</Example>

To count an uncountable thing, put a **container** or a **unit** before it: *a loaf of bread, a bottle of water, a glass of milk, a kilo of rice*. Many more of these (*a piece of, a slice of*) come in <LessonRef>B1 lesson 38 (Uncountable traps & a piece of)</LessonRef>.

<Example context="shopping" fa="یک نان و دو بطری آب لطفاً."><mark>A loaf of</mark> bread and <mark>two bottles of</mark> water, please.</Example>

Some words are uncountable in English but not in Persian: **advice, information, news, furniture, homework**. Say *some information*, not ~~some informations~~. These come in <LessonRef>B1 lesson 38 (Uncountable traps & a piece of)</LessonRef>. A few words, like *coffee*, can be both: *I like coffee* (the drink) and *Two coffees, please* (two cups). That comes in <LessonRef>B2 lesson 33 (a coffee or coffee? Nouns that change meaning)</LessonRef>.

## a / an or some?

- **One** countable thing → **a / an**: *I need **a** pen.*
- **Plural** countable → **some**: *I need **some** pens.*
- **Uncountable** → **some**: *I need **some** paper.*

<Example context="school" fa="یک خودکار و مقداری کاغذ لازم دارم.">I need <mark>a</mark> pen and <mark>some</mark> paper.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="کمی شیر، کمی نان و شش تا تخم‌مرغ لازم داریم.">We need <mark>some</mark> milk, <mark>some</mark> bread and six eggs.</Example>

**some** means "a number of" or "an amount of" when we don't say exactly how many or how much. Don't use *some* or *any* with **one** countable thing: ~~some pen~~, ~~any bank~~.

## some or any?

<FormTable caption="some and any">

| Sentence | Word | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive (+) | **some** | There are **some** eggs. There's **some** milk. |
| Negative (−) | **any** | There aren't **any** eggs. There isn't **any** milk. |
| Question (?) | **any** | Are there **any** eggs? Is there **any** milk? |

</FormTable>

<Example context="home" fa="برنج نداریم. (هیچ برنجی نیست.)">There isn't <mark>any</mark> rice.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="این نزدیکی مغازه‌ای هست؟">Are there <mark>any</mark> shops near the hotel?</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="سؤالی هست؟">Are there <mark>any</mark> questions?</Example>

**Offers and requests:** when you offer something or ask for something, use **some**, even in a question: *Would you like **some** tea? Can I have **some** water, please?* More on this in <LessonRef>A2 lesson 32 (some, any, no; something, anyone, nowhere)</LessonRef>.

**Things in general:** don't use *some* when you talk about a thing in general: *I like **cheese**.* (all cheese), not ~~I like some cheese~~. More in <LessonRef>A2 lesson 30 (the or no article?)</LessonRef>.

## How much? How many?

- **How many** + countable plural: *How many eggs? How many students are there?*
- **How much** + uncountable: *How much milk? How much time is there?*
- **How much is / are…?** asks the **price**: *How much is this bag? How much are these shoes?*

<Example context="school" fa="در کلاست چند دانشجو هست؟"><mark>How many</mark> students are there in your class?</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="در یخچال چقدر شیر هست؟"><mark>How much</mark> milk is there in the fridge?</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این چند است؟ (قیمت)"><mark>How much</mark> is this? (= What's the price?)</Example>

## a lot of

**a lot of** means "a big number" or "a big amount". It works with **both** countable and uncountable nouns. **lots of** means the same and is more informal.

<Example context="news" fa="امروز در مرکز شهر آدم‌های زیادی هستند.">There are <mark>a lot of</mark> people downtown today.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="امروز کار زیادی هست.">There's <mark>a lot of</mark> work today.</Example>

The verb follows the noun: *A lot of people **are** here.* (plural) · *A lot of sugar **is** bad for you.* (uncountable)

*much, many, a few* and *a little* come in <LessonRef>A2 lesson 31 (much, many, a lot of, a few, a little)</LessonRef>. *no* and *none* (*There's no milk. None left.*) come in <LessonRef>A2 lesson 32 (some, any, no; something, anyone, nowhere)</LessonRef>.

<Persian>

**قابل‌شمارش و غیرقابل‌شمارش:** بعضی اسم‌ها را می‌شمریم (an egg، two eggs) و بعضی را نه (milk، water، rice، money، bread).

- اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش **a / an** نمی‌گیرد و **جمع (s)** هم نمی‌شود.
- فعلش **مفرد** است: The rice **is** ready. (نه are)
- برای شمردن، از ظرف یا واحد استفاده می‌کنیم: a loaf of bread (یک نان)، a bottle of water (یک بطری آب)، a glass of milk (یک لیوان شیر).

**نکته برای فارسی‌زبان‌ها:** ما می‌گوییم «یک نان» یا «دو تا نان»، اما bread در انگلیسی غیرقابل‌شمارش است: some bread یا a loaf of bread. همین‌طور money هیچ‌وقت moneys نمی‌شود. «اطلاعات» در فارسی جمع است، ولی information در انگلیسی غیرقابل‌شمارش است و s نمی‌گیرد.

**some و any:** هر دو با اسم جمع یا غیرقابل‌شمارش می‌آیند.
- در جمله‌ی مثبت **some** (کمی / چندتا): There are some eggs.
- در جمله‌ی منفی و سؤالی **any**: There isn't any milk. / Are there any eggs? در فارسی می‌گوییم «هیچ شیری نیست»؛ any در جمله‌ی منفی کمی شبیه «هیچ» است.
- برای تعارف و درخواست، در سؤال هم **some**: Would you like some tea? (چای میل دارید؟)
- برای یک چیز قابل‌شمارش، a / an: I need a pen. (نه some pen)

**How much / How many:** How many برای چیزهای شمردنی (چند تا؟) و How much برای غیرشمردنی (چقدر؟). در فارسی برای هر دو می‌توانیم «چقدر» بگوییم، ولی در انگلیسی باید انتخاب کنیم. How much is it? یعنی «قیمتش چند است؟».

**a lot of** (خیلی / زیاد) برای هر دو نوع اسم: a lot of eggs، a lot of milk.

</Persian>

<MemoryHook title="The two-test">
