## Making a question

With **be**, a question is easy: move **am / is / are** to the front, before the subject.

<FormTable caption="be: statement → question">

| Statement | Question |
|---|---|
| I **am** late. | **Am** I late? |
| She **is** a nurse. | **Is** she a nurse? |
| It **is** expensive. | **Is** it expensive? |
| You **are** ready. | **Are** you ready? |
| They **are** at home. | **Are** they at home? |

</FormTable>

<Example context="travel" fa="ببخشید، این اتوبوسِ فرودگاه است؟"><mark>Is</mark> this the bus to the airport?</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="آماده‌ای؟ جلسه شروع شد.">Are you ready? The meeting is starting.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این کفش‌ها نو هستند؟"><mark>Are</mark> these shoes new?</Example>

## Short answers

In conversation we rarely answer with the whole sentence. We use a **short answer**: Yes / No + subject + **be**.

<FormTable caption="Short answers">

| Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| Are you a student? | Yes, I **am**. | No, I'**m not**. |
| Is he busy? | Yes, he **is**. | No, he **isn't**. |
| Is it far? | Yes, it **is**. | No, it **isn't**. |
| Are they here? | Yes, they **are**. | No, they **aren't**. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="«تشنه‌ای؟» «نه، نیستم.»">“Are you thirsty?” “<mark>No, I'm not.</mark>”</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="«سارا مریض است؟» «بله، هست.»">“Is Sara sick?” “<mark>Yes, she is.</mark>”</Example>

A **yes** short answer is never shortened: *Yes, I am* (not *Yes, I'm*), *Yes, she is* (not *Yes, she's*). A **no** answer is usually shortened: *No, she isn't.*

<Persian>

**سؤال با be:** در فارسی برای سؤال فقط لحن صدا را عوض می‌کنیم («خسته‌ای؟»). در انگلیسی **جای فعل عوض می‌شود**: am / is / are به اول جمله، قبل از فاعل، می‌رود.

- You are tired. ← Are you tired?
- She is at home. ← Is she at home?

**جواب کوتاه (short answer):** به جای تکرار کل جمله، می‌گوییم: Yes / No + فاعل + am / is / are.

- Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
- Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.

**نکته:** جواب مثبت کوتاه را هرگز خلاصه نکنید: Yes, I am (نه Yes, I'm).

</Persian>

<MemoryHook title="Jump to the front">
In a question, **be jumps to the front**: You are → **Are** you? In the answer, it **comes back**: Yes, I **am**.
</MemoryHook>
