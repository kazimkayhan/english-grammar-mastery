## Feelings in general: like, love, hate, enjoy + -ing

<FormTable caption="Verb + -ing">

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| **like** | I **like** walking in the park. |
| **love** | She **loves** cooking Persian food. |
| **hate** | They **hate** waiting in traffic. |
| **enjoy** | We **enjoy** traveling. |

</FormTable>

**like, love, hate** can also take **to** + verb (*I like to read*). **enjoy** takes only **-ing**.

<Example context="home" fa="مادرم عاشق آشپزی است.">My mother <mark>loves cooking</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="از سفر با قطار لذت می‌برم.">I <mark>enjoy traveling</mark> by train.</Example>

## Wants now: would like

**would like** = *want*, but more polite. The short form is **'d like**.

- I'd like + **noun**: *I'd like a coffee, please.*
- I'd like + **to** + verb: *I'd like to book a table.*
- **Would you like…?** = an offer: *Would you like some tea?*

<Example context="shopping" fa="یک کیلو سیب می‌خواستم، لطفاً.">I'<mark>d like</mark> a kilo of apples, please.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="چای میل دارید؟"><mark>Would you like</mark> some tea?</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="می‌خواستم با مدیر صحبت کنم.">I'<mark>d like to speak</mark> to the manager, please.</Example>

<Persian>

**like / love / hate / enjoy + ing:** برای احساس کلی درباره‌ی یک کار:

- I like reading (خواندن را دوست دارم)، I hate waiting (از انتظار متنفرم).
- enjoy فقط با ing می‌آید: I enjoy cooking (نه to cook).

**would like = خواستن، مؤدبانه.** در مغازه و رستوران به جای I want بگویید I'd like:

- I'd like a coffee. (یک قهوه می‌خواستم.)
- I'd like to book a table. (می‌خواستم یک میز رزرو کنم.)

**پیشنهاد:** Would you like some tea? (چای میل دارید؟)

**فرق مهم:** I like tea = کلاً چای دوست دارم. I'd like tea = الان چای می‌خواهم.

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="I want a coffee. (in a café: sounds rude)" right="I'd like a coffee, please." why="would like is the polite way to ask." />
<WrongRight wrong="Would you like coming with us?" right="Would you like to come with us?" why="would like + to + verb." />

<MemoryHook title="The little d is polite">
The little **'d** in **I'd like** turns a wish into a **polite request**. Add **please** and you sound perfect.
</MemoryHook>
