## Near or far? One or more?

<FormTable caption="this, that, these, those">

| | One (or not counted) | More than one |
|---|---|---|
| **Near** (here) | **this** book | **these** books |
| **Far** (there) | **that** house | **those** houses |

</FormTable>

<Example context="shopping" fa="این کیف چند است؟ و آن کفش‌ها؟">How much is <mark>this</mark> bag? And <mark>those</mark> shoes?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="آن ساختمان چیست؟">What's <mark>that</mark> building?</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="این قهوه سرد است."><mark>This</mark> coffee is cold.</Example>

Match the verb to the noun:

- **this / that** + one thing (or a thing we don't count, like *coffee*) + **is**: *This bag **is** nice.*
- **these / those** + plural noun + **are**: *These shoes **are** cheap.*

## With a noun or alone

**this, that, these** and **those** can go **before a noun** (*this book*) or stand **alone** (*What's this?*).

<Example context="home" fa="این‌ها کلیدهای من هستند."><mark>These</mark> are my keys.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="آن کیست؟ برادرت است؟">Who's <mark>that</mark>? Is <mark>that</mark> your brother?</Example>

When you **answer**, use **it** or **they**, not *this* or *these* again:

- “What's **this**?” “**It's** a phone charger.”
- “What are **those**?” “**They're** dates from Bam.”

## Introducing people and on the phone

To introduce one person, say **This is…**. For two or more, say **These are…**

<Example context="social" fa="این دوستم علی است."><mark>This is</mark> my friend Ali.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="این‌ها پدر و مادرم هستند."><mark>These are</mark> my parents.</Example>

On the phone, say your name with **this is**, not *I am*:

<Example context="work" fa="سلام، نیلوفر هستم، از دفتر.">Hello, <mark>this is</mark> Niloofar from the office.</Example>

## this and that for time

- **this** = now, today, the present week or year: *this morning, this week, this year*
- **that** = then, in the past: *that day, that night, that summer*

<Example context="work" fa="امروز صبح خیلی سرم شلوغ است.">I'm very busy <mark>this</mark> morning.</Example>
<Example context="news" fa="آن روز همه چیز تغییر کرد.">Everything changed <mark>that</mark> day.</Example>

## that for something just said

We often use **that** for something the other person **just said**:

<Example context="social" fa="«بیا برویم سینما.» «فکر خوبی است!»">“Let's go to the movies.” “<mark>That's</mark> a good idea!”</Example>

Other short replies: *That's right.* · *That's great!* · *That's OK.*

To say *this one* or *that one* (*I like that one*), see A2 lesson 35 (one, ones; another, other, the other).

<Persian>

**this / these / that / those:**

- this = این (نزدیک، یکی) ← this book
- these = این‌ها / این … ها (نزدیک، چندتا) ← these books
- that = آن (دور، یکی) ← that house
- those = آن‌ها / آن … ها (دور، چندتا) ← those houses

**نکته‌ی مهم:** در فارسی «این» و «آن» قبل از اسم جمع تغییر نمی‌کنند: «این کتاب‌ها»، «آن خانه‌ها». در انگلیسی برای جمع باید **these** یا **those** بگویید: these books (نه this books)، those houses (نه that houses). فعل هم عوض می‌شود: This book **is** new ← These books **are** new.

**معرفی:** «این دوستم علی است» = This is my friend Ali. «این‌ها پدر و مادرم هستند» = These are my parents.

**جواب سؤال:** What's this? ← It's a phone. (در جواب از it استفاده می‌کنیم.) What are those? ← They're dates.

**تلفن:** «سلام، سارا هستم» = Hello, this is Sara. (نه Hello, I am Sara)

**زمان:** this morning = امروز صبح، this week = این هفته، that day = آن روز (در گذشته).

**جواب به حرف دیگران:** «فکر خوبیه!» = That's a good idea!

</Persian>
