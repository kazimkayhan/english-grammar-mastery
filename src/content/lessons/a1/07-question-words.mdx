## The question words

<FormTable caption="Question words">

| Word | Asks about | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **what** | a thing, information | What's your name? |
| **where** | a place | Where is the station? |
| **who** | a person | Who is your teacher? |
| **when** | a time | When is the meeting? |
| **why** | a reason | Why are you late? |
| **how** | the way, a condition | How are you? |

</FormTable>

## how + another word

**how** joins with another word to ask about age, price, number and distance:

- **How old** are you? · I'm 30.
- **How much** is this coffee? · It's $3.
- **How many** children do they have? · Two.
- **How far** is the airport? · It's 20 kilometers.

<Example context="shopping" fa="این ژاکت چند است؟"><mark>How much</mark> is this jacket?</Example>

## The word order

The question word comes **first**. With **be**, the order is: question word + **be** + subject.

- **Where are you** from? (not *Where you are from?*)
- **What is** your phone number?

With other verbs we use **do / does**: *Where **do you** live?* You'll practice this in lesson A1.18.

**کلمات پرسشی:** what (چه / چی)، where (کجا)، who (چه کسی / کی)، when (چه وقت / کی)، why (چرا)، how (چطور).

**با how:** how old (چند سال)، how much (چقدر؛ برای قیمت و چیزهای غیرقابل‌شمارش)، how many (چند تا؛ برای چیزهای قابل‌شمارش)، how far (چقدر دور).

**ترتیب کلمات:** کلمه‌ی پرسشی همیشه **اول** جمله می‌آید و بعد از آن **فعل** و بعد **فاعل**:

- Where are you from? (نه Where you are from?)

در فارسی می‌گوییم «تو اهل کجایی؟» و کلمه‌ی پرسشی وسط یا آخر جمله است، اما در انگلیسی همیشه اول است. جواب why معمولاً با because شروع می‌شود.
