## The six question words

A question word asks for **information**. You can't answer it with *yes* or *no*.

<FormTable caption="Question words">

| Word | Asks about | Example | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **what** | a thing, information | What's your name? | Zahra. |
| **who** | a person | Who is your teacher? | Mr. Ahmadi. |
| **where** | a place | Where is the station? | On Park Street. |
| **when** | a time | When is the party? | On Friday. |
| **why** | a reason | Why are you tired? | Because I work at night. |
| **how** | the way, how someone is | How are you? | Fine, thanks. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="اسمت چیست؟ اهل کجایی؟"><mark>What</mark>'s your name? <mark>Where</mark> are you from?</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="مدیر جدید کیست؟"><mark>Who</mark> is the new manager?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="پرواز کی است؟"><mark>When</mark> is the flight?</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="مادرت حالش چطور است؟"><mark>How</mark> is your mother?</Example>

We answer with the information. A short answer is fine: “Where are you from?” “**Afghanistan.**” or “**I'm from Afghanistan.**”

## what + noun

**what** can go before a noun. Two very common questions:

- **What time** is the meeting? · At ten. (*what time* = the time on the clock)
- **What color** is your car? · Blue.

**when** asks about any time: a day, a month, a year or a clock time. **what time** asks only about the clock.

<Example context="work" fa="جلسه ساعت چند است؟"><mark>What time</mark> is the meeting?</Example>

## how

**how** has two simple uses:

- **How someone is** (health and feelings): *How are you?* · *Fine, thanks.*
- **The way** to do something: *How do you spell your name?*

<Example context="school" fa="اسمت را چطور می‌نویسی؟"><mark>How</mark> do you spell your name?</Example>

## how + another word

**how** joins with another word to ask about age, price, number, distance and size.

<FormTable caption="how + word">

| Question | Asks about | Example | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **how old** | age | How old are you? | I'm 30. |
| **how much** | a price, an amount | How much is this coffee? | It's $3. |
| **how many** | a number (+ plural noun) | How many children do they have? | Two. |
| **how far** | distance | How far is the airport? | About 20 kilometers. |
| **how big** | size | How big is your apartment? | It's small. |

</FormTable>

Three things to remember:

- **Age** uses **how old** + **be**: *How old are you?* Never ~~How many years do you have?~~
- **Price**: *How much **is** it?* for one thing, *How much **are** they?* for two or more.
- **how many** goes with a plural noun you can count: *How many **brothers**?* **how much** goes with things you don't count, like *money* or *water*: *How much money do you have?* <LessonRef>Lesson 15 (Countable & uncountable nouns; some, any, how much, how many)</LessonRef> explains this fully.

<Example context="shopping" fa="این ژاکت چند است؟"><mark>How much</mark> is this jacket?</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="چند تا دانشجو در کلاست هست؟"><mark>How many</mark> students are in your class?</Example>

## Word order with be

The question word comes **first**. Then **be** comes **before** the subject.

<FormTable caption="Question word + be + subject">

| Question word | be | Subject | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | **are** | you | from? |
| What | **is** | your phone number? | |
| Who | **is** | that woman? | |
| How old | **is** | your son? | |
| How much | **are** | these shoes? | |

</FormTable>

- **Where are you** from? (not ~~Where you are from?~~)
- **What is** your name? (not ~~What your name is?~~)

In speech, **is** is often short after a question word: **What's**, **Where's**, **Who's**, **How's**. With **are**, use the full form in writing: *Where are you from?*

<Example context="travel" fa="ایستگاه بس کجاست؟"><mark>Where's</mark> the bus station?</Example>

## Word order with other verbs: do / does

When the verb is **not be** (live, work, like…), we add **do** or **does** before the subject. The main verb comes after the subject, with no ending.

<FormTable caption="Question word + do / does + subject + verb">

| Question word | do / does | Subject | Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | **do** | you | live? |
| What | **do** | they | want? |
| When | **does** | the bank | open? |
| What | **does** | she | do? |

</FormTable>

- **does** goes with he, she, it (and one person or thing). **do** goes with I, you, we, they.
- After *does*, the verb has no **-s**: *Where does she **live**?* (not ~~Where does she lives?~~)
- *What do you do?* asks about someone's **job**: *I'm a nurse.*

You'll practice *do* and *does* in detail in <LessonRef>lesson 18 (Present simple: negatives & questions)</LessonRef>.

<Example context="work" fa="کجا کار می‌کنی؟">Where <mark>do you</mark> work?</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="بانک کی باز می‌شود؟">When <mark>does</mark> the bank open?</Example>

## why and because

We usually answer **why** with **because**.

<Example context="school" fa="«چرا دیر کردی؟» «چون ترافیک سنگین است.»">“<mark>Why</mark> are you late?” “<mark>Because</mark> the traffic is bad.”</Example>

**Why not?** is a short reply to a negative sentence: “I'm not hungry.” “**Why not?**”
