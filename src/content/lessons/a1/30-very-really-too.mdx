## very and really: stronger

<Example context="social" fa="او خیلی مهربان است.">She's <mark>very</mark> kind.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="اصفهان واقعاً زیباست.">Isfahan is <mark>really</mark> beautiful.</Example>

## too: more than is good

**too** means *more than is good, possible or needed*. There is always a **problem**:

<Example context="shopping" fa="این ماشین خیلی گران است؛ نمی‌توانم بخرمش.">This car is <mark>too</mark> expensive. I can't buy it.</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="چای خیلی داغ است؛ صبر کن.">The tea is <mark>too</mark> hot. Wait a minute.</Example>

<FormTable caption="very or too?">

| | Meaning | Result |
|---|---|---|
| The tea is **very** hot. | hotter than usual | Drink it carefully. (OK) |
| The tea is **too** hot. | more than is good | You can't drink it. (a problem) |

</FormTable>

<Persian>

**very / really = خیلی (مثبت یا خنثی):** It's very cold (خیلی سرد است). really بیشتر در گفتار می‌آید.

**too = بیش از حد:** وقتی «خیلی» یک **مشکل** ایجاد می‌کند، از too استفاده کنید:

- It's too expensive. (خیلی گران است ← نمی‌توانم بخرم.)
- She's too young to drive. (برای رانندگی خیلی جوان است.)

**نکته‌ی مهم:** «خیلی» فارسی هم very است و هم too. از خودتان بپرسید: «آیا مشکلی هست؟» اگر بله ← too. اگر نه ← very.

مثلاً «اینجا خیلی قشنگ است!» = It's very beautiful here (نه too beautiful، چون مشکلی نیست).

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="I love this city. It's too beautiful." right="I love this city. It's very beautiful." why="A positive feeling, no problem → very. too means a problem." />
<WrongRight wrong="It's very late. We can't catch the bus." right="It's too late. We can't catch the bus." why="There's a problem → too." />

<MemoryHook title="Too = trouble">
**Too** and **trouble** both start with **t**: if there's trouble, say **too**. No trouble? Say **very**.
</MemoryHook>
