## The past of be

In the present, **be** has three forms (*am, is, are*, <LessonRef>lesson 5</LessonRef>). In the past it has only two: **was** and **were**.

<FormTable caption="be: past simple">

| Subject | Present | Past | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | am | **was** | I **was** at home. |
| he / she / it | is | **was** | She **was** tired. |
| you / we / they | are | **were** | They **were** late. |

</FormTable>

Three things to remember:

- **You** always takes **were**, even for one person: *You were right, Sam.*
- A **plural noun** takes **were**: *The rooms were clean.* Two names are plural too: *Ahmad and Karim were at work.*
- Positive **was** and **were** have no short form. Write *I was*, *they were*. (Short forms are only for the negative: *wasn't, weren't*.)

<Timeline items={[{ type: "point", at: -0.55, label: "was / were" }]} caption="A state at a finished time in the past." />

<Example context="health" fa="دیروز خانه بودم. مریض بودم.">I <mark>was</mark> at home yesterday. I <mark>was</mark> sick.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="هتل گران نبود و اتاق‌ها تمیز بودند.">The hotel <mark>wasn't</mark> expensive, and the rooms <mark>were</mark> clean.</Example>

## What we use was / were for

Use **was / were** for the same things as *am / is / are*, but in the past:

- **Place**: *I was at home. The keys were on the table.*
- **Feelings and states**: *We were hungry. She was sick.*
- **Name and job**: *My grandfather was a teacher.*
- **Age**: *I was ten in 2010.* Age uses **be**, never *have*.
- **Description**: *The film was boring. The questions were easy.*
- **Time and weather** (with *it*): *It was Monday. It was very cold.* (More in <LessonRef>lesson 34, *it for time, weather & distance*</LessonRef>.)

<Example context="home" fa="پدرکلانم معلم بود.">My grandfather <mark>was</mark> a teacher.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="سؤال‌های امتحان آسان بودند.">The exam questions <mark>were</mark> easy.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="سال ۲۰۱۰ ده ساله بودم.">I <mark>was</mark> ten in 2010.</Example>

## Negatives

Put **not** after *was* or *were*. In speech we usually use the short forms.

<FormTable caption="was / were: negative">

| Full negative | Short negative |
|---|---|
| I / he / she / it **was not** | **wasn't** |
| you / we / they **were not** | **weren't** |

</FormTable>

**Never use *did* with be.** Say **I wasn't at home**, not ~~I didn't be at home~~. (*Did* is for other verbs: *I didn't go*. That is <LessonRef>lesson 27, *Past simple: irregular verbs & did*</LessonRef>.)

<Example context="work" fa="دیروز سر کار نبودم.">I <mark>wasn't</mark> at work yesterday.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="کفش‌ها گران نبودند.">The shoes <mark>weren't</mark> expensive.</Example>

## Questions and short answers

To ask a yes/no question, **swap** the subject and the verb: *You were at home* → **Were you at home?** Again, no *did*.

<FormTable caption="was / were: questions and short answers">

| Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| **Was** I late? | Yes, you **were**. | No, you **weren't**. |
| **Was** she at school? | Yes, she **was**. | No, she **wasn't**. |
| **Were** you at home? | Yes, I **was**. | No, I **wasn't**. |
| **Were** they tired? | Yes, they **were**. | No, they **weren't**. |

</FormTable>

Look at the third line: a question with **you** often gets an answer with **I** (or **we**), so *Were you…?* → *Yes, I **was**.*

For a question with a question word, use **question word + was / were + subject**:

<Example context="social" fa="دیشب کجا بودی؟">Where <mark>were you</mark> last night?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="سفرت چطور بود؟">How <mark>was your trip</mark>?</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="چرا امروز دیر کردی؟">Why <mark>were you</mark> late today?</Example>

## there was / there were

**there was / there were** is the past of *there is / there are* (<LessonRef>lesson 14</LessonRef>). Use it to say that something existed in the past.

- **there was** + one thing or an uncountable noun: *There was a bank. There was a lot of traffic.*
- **there were** + two or more things: *There were two banks.*

<FormTable caption="there was / there were">

| | One thing / uncountable | Two or more |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | There **was** a bank. | There **were** two banks. |
| Negative | There **wasn't** a bank. | There **weren't** any banks. |
| Question | **Was there** a bank? | **Were there** any banks? |
| Short answer | Yes, there **was**. / No, there **wasn't**. | Yes, there **were**. / No, there **weren't**. |

</FormTable>

Don't leave out **there**: say **There was a lot of traffic**, not ~~Was a lot of traffic~~.

<Example context="news" fa="دیروز ترافیک زیادی بود و دو تصادف رخ داد.">There <mark>was</mark> a lot of traffic yesterday, and there <mark>were</mark> two accidents.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="نزدیک هتل رستوران‌های خوبی بود؟"><mark>Were there</mark> any good restaurants near the hotel?</Example>

## be born

Birth is always in the past, so we say **was / were born**. Not ~~I am born~~ and not ~~I born~~.

- **in** + a year or a place: *I was born **in** 1995. I was born **in** Herat.*
- **on** + a date: *I was born **on** May 15, 1995.* (More about *in* and *on* in <LessonRef>lesson 32, *Prepositions of time: at, on, in*</LessonRef>.)

To ask, use **Where / When + was / were + subject + born?**

<Example context="social" fa="من سال ۱۹۹۵ در مزار شریف به دنیا آمدم.">I <mark>was born</mark> in Mazar-i-Sharif in 1995.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="کجا به دنیا آمدی؟">Where <mark>were you born</mark>?</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="پسرشان هفته‌ی پیش به دنیا آمد.">Their son <mark>was born</mark> last week.</Example>

## Other past forms

In this lesson **was / were** is the main verb. Other verbs have their own past forms: *I worked, I went.* You learn them in <LessonRef>lesson 26 (Past simple: regular verbs)</LessonRef> and <LessonRef>lesson 27 (Past simple: irregular verbs & did)</LessonRef>. *Was* and *were* are also helpers with **-ing** verbs: *I was working.* That is the past continuous, in <LessonRef>A2 lesson 8 (Past continuous; when & while)</LessonRef>.

<Persian>

**گذشته‌ی فعل be (بودن):** در زمان حال، be سه شکل داشت (am / is / are). در گذشته فقط **دو شکل** دارد:

- با **I / he / she / it** ← **was**: I was tired. (خسته بودم.) / She was at home. (او خانه بود.)
- با **you / we / they** ← **were**: We were in Band-e-Amir. (ما بند امیر بودیم.)
- هر **اسم جمع** هم با **were** می‌آید: The rooms were clean. (اتاق‌ها تمیز بودند.)

**چرا اشتباه می‌کنیم؟** در فارسی «بودن» شش شکل دارد (بودم، بودی، بود، بودیم، بودید، بودند). در انگلیسی فقط دو شکل داریم. ساده به خاطر بسپارید: am و is ← **was**، و are ← **were**. پس They was و You was غلط است.

**کاربرد:** هر جا در حال am / is / are می‌گوییم، در گذشته was / were می‌گوییم: برای مکان (I was at home)، احساس (We were hungry)، شغل (He was a teacher)، سن (I was ten) و توصیف (The film was boring). در حالت مثبت، was و were حالت کوتاه ندارند.

**منفی:** not را بعد از was / were می‌گذاریم: wasn't (was not) و weren't (were not). برای be **هرگز did نمی‌آوریم**: I wasn't at home. (خانه نبودم.) نه I didn't be at home.

**سؤالی:** در فارسی سؤال را فقط با لحن می‌سازیم («دیروز خانه بودی؟») و ترتیب کلمه‌ها عوض نمی‌شود. در انگلیسی was / were را **قبل از فاعل** می‌آوریم: Were you at home yesterday? با کلمه‌ی پرسشی هم همین‌طور: Where were you? (کجا بودی؟) نه Where you were?

**جواب کوتاه:** Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. — Yes, they were. / No, they weren't. دقت کنید: سؤال با you است، ولی جواب با I: Were you tired? ← Yes, I was.

**there was / there were:** گذشته‌ی there is / there are است و یعنی «… بود / … وجود داشت». there was برای یک چیز (یا اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش مثل traffic)، و there were برای دو یا چند چیز. دو اشتباه رایج:

- در فارسی می‌گوییم «ترافیک زیادی بود» و کلمه‌ای مثل there نداریم. در انگلیسی there لازم است: There was a lot of traffic. نه Was a lot of traffic.
- در فارسی برای چیزهای جمع گاهی فعل مفرد می‌آوریم («دو تا تصادف بود»). در انگلیسی برای جمع حتماً were لازم است: There were two accidents.

**تولد:** در فارسی می‌گوییم «متولد ۱۹۹۵ هستم» یا «سال ۱۹۹۵ به دنیا آمدم». در انگلیسی همیشه **was / were born** می‌گوییم: I was born in 1995. نه I am born و نه I born.

- **in** + سال یا شهر: I was born in 1995. / I was born in Herat.
- **on** + تاریخ کامل: I was born on May 15, 1995.
- سؤال: Where were you born? (کجا به دنیا آمدی؟) / When were you born? (کی به دنیا آمدی؟)

<MemoryHook title="One was, many were">
**One** person or thing (I, he, she, it) → **was**. **You** and **many** (we, they, plural nouns) → **were**. And no *did*: just swap for a question and add *not* for a negative.
</MemoryHook>
