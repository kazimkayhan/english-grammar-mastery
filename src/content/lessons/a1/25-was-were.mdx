## The past of be

<FormTable caption="was / were">

| | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / he / she / it | I **was** tired. | She **wasn't** tired. | **Was** he tired? |
| you / we / they | They **were** late. | We **weren't** late. | **Were** you late? |

</FormTable>

Short answers: *Yes, I **was**. / No, they **weren't**.*

<Timeline items={[{ type: "point", at: -0.55, label: "was / were" }]} caption="A state at a finished time in the past." />

<Example context="health" fa="دیروز خانه بودم. مریض بودم.">I <mark>was</mark> at home yesterday. I <mark>was</mark> sick.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="دیشب کجا بودی؟">Where <mark>were</mark> you last night?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="هتل گران نبود و اتاق‌ها تمیز بودند.">The hotel <mark>wasn't</mark> expensive, and the rooms <mark>were</mark> clean.</Example>

## there was / there were

<Example context="news" fa="دیروز ترافیک زیادی بود و دو تصادف رخ داد.">There <mark>was</mark> a lot of traffic yesterday, and there <mark>were</mark> two accidents.</Example>

## be born

We always say **was / were born**, because birth is in the past:

<Example context="social" fa="من در سال ۱۹۹۵ در اصفهان به دنیا آمدم.">I <mark>was born</mark> in Isfahan in 1995.</Example>

<Persian>

**گذشته‌ی be:** am و is ← **was**، are ← **were**.

- I / he / she / it ← was: I was tired (خسته بودم).
- you / we / they ← were: They were late (دیر کردند / دیر آمده بودند).

**منفی:** wasn't / weren't — **سؤالی:** Was he…? / Were you…?

**there was / there were:** گذشته‌ی there is / there are (… بود / … بودند).

**تولد:** در فارسی می‌گوییم «متولد ۱۹۹۵ هستم»، ولی در انگلیسی همیشه گذشته است: **I was born** in 1995 (نه I am born).

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="You was right." right="You were right." why="you always takes were." />
<WrongRight wrong="Where you were yesterday?" right="Where were you yesterday?" why="Question: were before the subject." />

<MemoryHook title="One was, many were">
**One** person (I, he, she, it) → **was**. You and **many** people → **were**.
</MemoryHook>
