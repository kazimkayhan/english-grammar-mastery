## The form

<FormTable caption="Present continuous">

| | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I**'m** work**ing** | I**'m not** working | **Am** I working? |
| he / she / it | She**'s** work**ing** | She **isn't** working | **Is** she working? |
| you / we / they | They**'re** work**ing** | They **aren't** working | **Are** they working? |

</FormTable>

## Spelling the -ing form

<FormTable caption="-ing spelling">

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| most verbs: + **ing** | work → work**ing** · play → play**ing** |
| silent **-e**: drop it | make → mak**ing** · write → writ**ing** |
| one vowel + one consonant: **double** it | run → run**ning** · swim → swim**ming** · sit → sit**ting** |
| **-ie** → **-ying** | lie → l**ying** · die → d**ying** |

</FormTable>

<Persian>

**حال استمراری (present continuous):** برای کاری که **همین الان** یا **این روزها** در جریان است. معادل «دارم… می‌کنم» در فارسی.

**ساختار:** am / is / are + فعل + **ing**

- I'm cooking. (دارم آشپزی می‌کنم.)
- She's sleeping. (خواب است / دارد می‌خوابد.)

**املای ing:** make ← making (حذف e)، run ← running (دو بار n)، lie ← lying.

**اشتباه رایج:** am / is / are را جا نیندازید: She **is** cooking (نه She cooking).

**کلمه‌های نشانه:** now، right now، at the moment، today، this week.

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="I writting an email." right="I'm writing an email." why="Add am, and write → writing (one t)." />
<WrongRight wrong="He is play football." right="He is playing football." why="be + verb-ing." />

<MemoryHook title="Two pieces">
The present continuous always has **two pieces**: **be** (am / is / are) + **-ing**. One piece missing = mistake.
</MemoryHook>
