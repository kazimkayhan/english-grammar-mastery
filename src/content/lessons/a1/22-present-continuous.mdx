## The form

The present continuous has **two pieces**: **am / is / are** (the verb *be*, <LessonRef>lesson 5</LessonRef>) + the verb with **-ing**.

<FormTable caption="Present continuous">

| | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I**'m** work**ing** | I**'m not** working | **Am** I working? |
| he / she / it | She**'s** work**ing** | She **isn't** working / She**'s not** working | **Is** she working? |
| you / we / they | They**'re** work**ing** | They **aren't** working / They**'re not** working | **Are** they working? |

</FormTable>

In speech we usually use the short forms (*I'm, she's, they're*). The full forms (*I am working*) are also correct.

<Example context="travel" fa="دارم رانندگی می‌کنم. دارم به خانه می‌آیم.">I<mark>'m driving</mark>. I<mark>'m coming</mark> home.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="هیس! بچه خواب است.">Shh! The baby <mark>is sleeping</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="الان کار نمی‌کنم. در استراحتم.">I<mark>'m not working</mark> now. I'm on my break.</Example>

## Questions and short answers

Put **am / is / are** before the subject. For a wh- question, put the question word first.

<FormTable caption="Questions">

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| yes/no | **Am / Is / Are** + subject + -ing? | **Are** you listening? |
| short answers | Yes, + subject + **am / is / are**. No, + subject + **'m not / isn't / aren't**. | Yes, I **am**. / No, I**'m not**. |
| wh- | question word + **am / is / are** + subject + -ing? | **What are** you doing? |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="«داری گوش می‌دهی؟» «بله، گوش می‌دهم.»">“<mark>Are</mark> you <mark>listening</mark>?” “Yes, I <mark>am</mark>.”</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="«باران می‌آید؟» «نه، نمی‌آید.»">“<mark>Is</mark> it <mark>raining</mark>?” “No, it <mark>isn't</mark>.”</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="کجا می‌روی؟"><mark>Where are</mark> you <mark>going</mark>?</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="چرا گریه می‌کند؟"><mark>Why is</mark> she <mark>crying</mark>?</Example>

The short answer uses only *be*: *Yes, I am*, not ~~Yes, I am listening~~ (that is correct but too long) and never ~~Yes, I'm~~.

## Spelling the -ing form

<FormTable caption="-ing spelling">

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| most verbs: just add **-ing** | work → work**ing** · play → play**ing** · study → study**ing** |
| silent **-e** at the end: drop it | make → mak**ing** · write → writ**ing** · come → com**ing** |
| but **-ee**, and **be**: keep it | see → see**ing** · agree → agree**ing** · be → be**ing** |
| one vowel + one consonant at the end: **double** the consonant | run → run**ning** · swim → swim**ming** · sit → sit**ting** · stop → stop**ping** |
| **-ie** → **-ying** | lie → l**ying** · die → d**ying** · tie → t**ying** |

</FormTable>

Two more things about doubling:

- Don't double **w**, **x** or **y**: *snow → snowing, fix → fixing, play → playing*.
- In longer verbs, double only when the **last part is stressed**: *beGIN → beginning*, but *VISit → visiting*, *LISten → listening*, *HAPpen → happening*.

<UkNote>British English doubles a final **l** after one vowel: **travelling**. American English doesn't when the stress is at the start: *traveling*.</UkNote>

## Now and around now

**1. Now: at this moment.** The action has started and it isn't finished.

<Timeline items={[{ type: "wave", from: -0.25, to: 0.25, label: "now" }]} caption="The action is in progress at the moment of speaking." />

<Example context="home" fa="دارم ظرف می‌شویم. نمی‌توانم تلفن را جواب بدهم.">I<mark>'m washing</mark> the dishes. I can't answer the phone.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="ببین! معلم دارد می‌آید.">Look! The teacher <mark>is coming</mark>.</Example>

**2. Around now: this week, these days.** The action is in progress around this time, but maybe not at this exact moment.

<Timeline items={[{ type: "span", from: -0.5, to: 0.5, label: "this week" }]} caption="In progress around now, for a limited time." />

<Example context="work" fa="این هفته روی یک پروژه‌ی جدید کار می‌کنیم.">We<mark>'re working</mark> on a new project this week.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="این روزها یک کتاب خیلی خوب می‌خوانم.">I<mark>'m reading</mark> a really good book these days.</Example>

Maybe I'm not reading at this exact moment, but I'm in the middle of the book.

**Time words** we often use: *now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, this month, these days*. And **Look!** or **Listen!** tell you it is happening now: *Listen! Someone is singing.*

## What comes next

- Some verbs (*know, like, want, need*) don't usually take **-ing**: say *I know*, not ~~I'm knowing~~. <LessonRef>Lesson 23</LessonRef>, Present simple or present continuous?, shows when to use each present tense, with temporary situations and changes (*It's getting colder*).
- The present continuous can also talk about plans: *I'm meeting Ali at six.* That comes in <LessonRef>A2 lesson 13</LessonRef>, Present continuous for the future.

**حال استمراری (present continuous):** برای کاری که **همین الان** یا **این روزها** در جریان است. معادل «دارم… می‌کنم» در فارسی.

**ساختار:** am / is / are + فعل + **ing**

- I'm cooking. (دارم آشپزی می‌کنم.)
- She's sleeping. (خواب است.)
- They're playing football. (دارند فوتبال بازی می‌کنند.)

**منفی:** not بعد از am / is / are: I'm not working. / She isn't working. / They aren't working.

**سؤالی:** am / is / are را قبل از فاعل می‌گذاریم: Are you working? (داری کار می‌کنی؟) What are you doing? (داری چه کار می‌کنی؟) جواب کوتاه: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

**دو کاربرد:**

- **همین الان:** I'm washing the dishes. (دارم ظرف می‌شویم.)
- **این روزها (موقت):** I'm reading a good book this week. (این هفته دارم یک کتاب خوب می‌خوانم.) شاید همین لحظه نخوانم، ولی وسط خواندن کتاب هستم.

**املای ing:**

- معمولاً فقط ing: work ← working
- e آخر را حذف کنید: make ← making (اما see ← seeing و be ← being)
- یک حرف صدادار + یک بی‌صدا در آخر: حرف آخر دو بار: run ← running، swim ← swimming
- ie ← y: lie ← lying
- w، x و y دو بار نمی‌شوند: play ← playing

**چرا فارسی‌زبان‌ها اشتباه می‌کنند؟**

- در فارسی «می‌خورم» هم برای عادت است و هم برای الان. پس فارسی‌زبان برای الان هم می‌گوید I eat lunch now. در انگلیسی برای الان بگویید **I'm eating** lunch now.
- در «دارم می‌خورم» فعل «بودن» وجود ندارد، پس فارسی‌زبان‌ها am / is / are را جا می‌اندازند: She cooking. درست: She **is** cooking.
- بعضی‌ها ing را فراموش می‌کنند: He is play. درست: He is **playing**. هر دو قطعه لازم است: be + ing.

**کلمه‌های نشانه:** now، right now، at the moment، today، this week، these days، Look!، Listen!

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="I writting an email." right="I'm writing an email." why="Add am, and write → writing (one t)." />
<WrongRight wrong="He is play football." right="He is playing football." why="be + verb-ing." />
<WrongRight wrong="She cooking dinner." right="She's cooking dinner." why="Don't forget be (is)." />
<WrongRight wrong="Look! It rains." right="Look! It's raining." why="Look! = now → present continuous." />
<WrongRight wrong="What you are doing?" right="What are you doing?" why="In a question, be goes before the subject." />
<WrongRight wrong="They are siting in the garden." right="They are sitting in the garden." why="sit: one vowel + one consonant → double the t." />

<MemoryHook title="Two pieces">
The present continuous always has **two pieces**: **be** (am / is / are) + **-ing**. One piece missing = mistake.
</MemoryHook>
