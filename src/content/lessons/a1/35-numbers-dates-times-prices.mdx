## Cardinal numbers

<FormTable caption="Saying numbers">

| Number | Say | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 13 / 30 | thir**teen** / **thir**ty | Don't mix them up: stress -TEEN, but THIR-ty |
| 21 | twenty**-**one | hyphen between tens and units |
| 100 | **a** hundred / **one** hundred | |
| 350 | three hundred **and** fifty (UK) · three hundred fifty (US) | |
| 2,000 | two **thousand** | no -s: two thousand (not thousands) |
| 1,000,000 | a / one **million** | |
| 2.5 | two **point** five | a point, not a comma |

</FormTable>

**hundred, thousand, million** take no -s after a number: *three hundred people*. (Use **hundreds of** / **thousands of** without a number: *hundreds of people*.)

## Ordinal numbers

<FormTable caption="Ordinals">

| Number | Ordinal | Number | Ordinal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | **first** | 8th | eigh**th** |
| 2nd | **second** | 9th | **ninth** |
| 3rd | **third** | 12th | **twelfth** |
| 4th | fourth | 20th | twent**ieth** |
| 5th | **fifth** | 21st | twenty-**first** |
| 6th | sixth | 100th | hundredth |

</FormTable>

Use ordinals for dates, floors, birthdays and order: *the **second** floor, my **thirtieth** birthday*.

## Dates and years

<FormTable caption="Dates">

| | US | UK |
|---|---|---|
| Write | May 5, 2024 · 5/5/2024 | 5 May 2024 · 5/5/2024 |
| Say | May fifth | the fifth of May |
| Watch out! | 3/4 = **March 4** | 3/4 = **3 April** |

</FormTable>

<FormTable caption="Years">

| Year | Say |
|---|---|
| 1998 | nineteen ninety-eight |
| 2005 | two thousand (and) five |
| 2024 | twenty twenty-four (or two thousand (and) twenty-four) |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="تولد من بیست و یکم مه است.">My birthday is on May <mark>twenty-first</mark>.</Example>
<Example context="news" fa="او در سال ۱۹۹۸ به دنیا آمد.">She was born in <mark>nineteen ninety-eight</mark>.</Example>

## Telling the time

<FormTable caption="Times">

| Time | Easy way | Traditional way |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00 | seven | seven **o'clock** |
| 7:05 | seven oh five | five **past** seven |
| 7:15 | seven fifteen | a quarter **past** seven (US also: **after**) |
| 7:30 | seven thirty | **half past** seven |
| 7:45 | seven forty-five | a quarter **to** eight |
| 7:50 | seven fifty | ten **to** eight |

</FormTable>

- **o'clock** is only for full hours: *seven o'clock* (not *seven thirty o'clock*).
- **a.m.** = before noon, **p.m.** = after noon: *9 a.m., 6 p.m.*

<Example context="work" fa="قطار ساعت یک ربع به نه حرکت می‌کند.">The train leaves at <mark>a quarter to nine</mark>.</Example>

## Prices

<Example context="shopping" fa="«این چند است؟» «دوازده دلار و پنجاه سنت.»">“How much is it?” “It's <mark>twelve fifty</mark>.” ($12.50)</Example>

- **$3.50** = three fifty · three dollars and fifty cents
- **£20** = twenty pounds · **€5.99** = five ninety-nine
- **50,000 tomans** = fifty thousand tomans

<Persian>

**اعداد:**

- بیست و یک = twenty-one (با خط تیره، بدون and).
- بعد از عدد، hundred و thousand جمع نمی‌شوند: three hundred (نه three hundreds).
- **ممیز و جداکننده برعکس فارسی است:** در انگلیسی اعشار با نقطه (2.5) و هزارگان با کاما (10,000) نوشته می‌شود.

**اعداد ترتیبی** (اول، دوم، سوم): first، second، third، و بقیه معمولاً با th: fourth، fifth.

**تاریخ:** در آمریکایی May 5 (بگویید May fifth) و در بریتانیایی 5 May (بگویید the fifth of May). مراقب باشید: 3/4 در آمریکا یعنی چهارم مارس!

**سال:** دو رقم دو رقم خوانده می‌شود: 1998 = nineteen ninety-eight.

**ساعت:**

- شش و نیم = six thirty یا half past six
- نه و ربع = nine fifteen یا a quarter past nine
- یک ربع به نه = eight forty-five یا a quarter to nine
- o'clock فقط برای ساعت کامل: seven o'clock

**قیمت:** «چند است؟» = How much is it? و $3.50 = three fifty.

</Persian>

<MemoryHook title="Point vs comma: the opposite of Persian">
English uses a **point** for small parts (**2.5**) and a **comma** for big groups (**10,000**). If a number looks strange, try swapping them in your head.
</MemoryHook>
