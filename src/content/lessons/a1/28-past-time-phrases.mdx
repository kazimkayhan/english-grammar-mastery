## When did it happen?

<FormTable caption="Past time phrases">

| Phrase | Examples |
|---|---|
| **yesterday** (+ part of the day) | yesterday · yesterday morning · yesterday evening |
| **last** + time | last night · last week · last month · last year · last Friday |
| **in** + year / month | in 2019 · in March · in the summer of 2020 |
| time + **ago** | five minutes ago · two days ago · a long time ago |

</FormTable>

## No preposition with last and yesterday

*~~in last week~~* → **last week** · *~~at yesterday~~* → **yesterday**

## ago comes after the time

Persian says «دو ساعت **قبل**», and «قبل» usually means *before*, so learners say *~~before two hours~~*. English uses **ago**, after the time: **two hours ago**.

<Persian>

**عبارت‌های زمان گذشته:**

- **yesterday** = دیروز (yesterday morning = دیروز صبح)
- **last** + زمان = … گذشته: last week (هفته‌ی گذشته)، last night (دیشب)
- **in** + سال یا ماه: in 2019، in March
- زمان + **ago** = … پیش: two days ago (دو روز پیش)

**دو اشتباه رایج:**

۱. قبل از last و yesterday حرف اضافه نمی‌آید: last week (نه in last week).
۲. ago بعد از زمان می‌آید و before نیست: two hours ago (نه before two hours).

</Persian>
