## Two presents, two meanings

English has two present tenses. You know their forms from <LessonRef>lessons 17, 18 and 22</LessonRef>. Now the question is: **which one?**

<Timeline items={[{ type: "repeat", from: -0.9, to: 0.9, label: "simple: again and again" }, { type: "wave", from: -0.2, to: 0.2, label: "continuous: now" }]} caption="Present simple = repeated or always true. Present continuous = in progress around now." />

<FormTable caption="Present simple vs present continuous">

| Present simple | Present continuous |
|---|---|
| **Habits**: I **drink** tea every day. | **Now**: I'**m drinking** tea right now. |
| **Permanent**: I **live** in Shiraz. | **Temporary**: I'**m living** in Tehran this year. |
| **Facts**: Water **boils** at 100°C. | **Changes**: The water **is getting** hot. |
| **States**: I **know**, I **like**, I **want** | (not used with most state verbs) |
| always, usually, every day, on Mondays | now, at the moment, today, this week, Look! |

</FormTable>

## Habit or now?

The **present simple** is for things you do again and again (habits, routines) and for facts. The **present continuous** is for things in progress now.

<Example context="work" fa="معمولاً با ماشین سر کار می‌روم، ولی امروز با اتوبوس می‌روم.">I usually <mark>drive</mark> to work, but today I<mark>'m taking</mark> the bus.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="پدرم هر روز آشپزی می‌کند. الان دارد شام درست می‌کند.">My dad <mark>cooks</mark> every day. Right now he<mark>'s making</mark> dinner.</Example>
<Example context="news" fa="خورشید از شرق طلوع می‌کند.">The sun <mark>rises</mark> in the east. (a fact)</Example>

The two tenses ask different questions:

<FormTable caption="Two questions, two meanings">

| Question | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **What do you do?** | What's your job? | I'm a nurse. I work at a hospital. |
| **What are you doing?** | What are you doing now? | I'm cooking dinner. |
| **Do you watch** TV? | in general, a habit | Yes, every evening. |
| **Are you watching** TV? | now | No, I'm reading. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="«شغلت چیست؟» «معلمم.»">“What <mark>do</mark> you <mark>do</mark>?” “I'm a teacher.”</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="«داری چه کار می‌کنی؟» «دارم تکالیفم را انجام می‌دهم.»">“What <mark>are</mark> you <mark>doing</mark>?” “I<mark>'m doing</mark> my homework.”</Example>

## Permanent or temporary?

Use the **present simple** for situations that are permanent, or true for a long time. Use the **present continuous** for situations that are **temporary**: only for a short time, then they change.

<Example context="home" fa="من در شیراز زندگی می‌کنم.">I <mark>live</mark> in Shiraz. (my home)</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="این ماه پیش خاله‌ام می‌مانم.">I<mark>'m staying</mark> with my aunt this month. (just for now)</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="خواهرم در شیراز زندگی می‌کند، ولی این ترم در تهران درس می‌خواند.">My sister <mark>lives</mark> in Shiraz, but this term she<mark>'s studying</mark> in Tehran.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="او معمولاً در دفتر کار می‌کند، اما این هفته از خانه کار می‌کند.">He usually <mark>works</mark> in the office, but this week he<mark>'s working</mark> from home.</Example>

Words like **this week, this month, this year, these days, at the moment** often show that a situation is temporary.

## Changing situations

Use the **present continuous** for things that are **changing** now: getting bigger, smaller, better, worse, colder…

<Example context="home" fa="هوا دارد سرد می‌شود. ژاکتت را بپوش.">It<mark>'s getting</mark> cold. Put on your jacket.</Example>
<Example context="news" fa="قیمت‌ها دارد بالا می‌رود.">Prices <mark>are going</mark> up.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="انگلیسی‌ات دارد بهتر می‌شود!">Your English <mark>is improving</mark>!</Example>
<Example context="news" fa="شهر خیلی سریع دارد بزرگ می‌شود.">The city <mark>is growing</mark> very fast.</Example>

A common pattern is **be getting** + an adjective: *It's getting dark. The baby is getting tired. It's getting colder.*

## Verbs that don't like -ing

Some verbs are about **states** (what you know, feel, want or have), not actions. These **state verbs** are usually in the **present simple**, even when we mean *now*.

<FormTable caption="Common state verbs">

| Group | Verbs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| thinking | know, understand, believe, remember, mean | I **know** the answer. (not ~~I'm knowing~~) |
| feeling | like, love, hate, prefer | She **likes** this song. (not ~~She's liking~~) |
| wanting | want, need | I **want** a coffee now. (not ~~I'm wanting~~) |
| having | belong, own | This bag **belongs** to me. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="school" fa="الان می‌فهمم! ممنون.">Oh, I <mark>understand</mark> now! Thanks.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="یک گوشی جدید لازم دارم.">I <mark>need</mark> a new phone.</Example>

Some verbs, like **have** and **think**, can be a state or an action: *I **have** a car* (state) but *I'm **having** lunch* (action). That comes in <LessonRef>B1 lesson 10</LessonRef>, State verbs & action verbs.

**حال ساده یا حال استمراری؟**

- **حال ساده (present simple):** عادت (I drink tea every day = هر روز چای می‌نوشم)، حقیقت (Water boils at 100°C = آب در ۱۰۰ درجه می‌جوشد)، وضعیت دائمی (I live in Shiraz = در شیراز زندگی می‌کنم) و فعل‌های حالت.

- What do you do? = شغلت چیست؟

**چرا فارسی‌زبان‌ها اشتباه می‌کنند؟** در فارسی هر دو معنی را معمولاً با «می‌…» می‌گوییم: «چای **می‌**نوشم» هم یعنی «هر روز می‌نوشم» و هم «الان می‌نوشم». «دارم» را فقط گاهی برای «همین الان» اضافه می‌کنیم، و برای وضعیت موقت معمولاً نمی‌گوییم («این ماه پیش خاله‌ام **می**‌مانم»). پس فارسی‌زبان‌ها معمولاً حال ساده را جای حال استمراری به کار می‌برند. همیشه به **زمان** فکر کنید:

- هر روز؟ همیشه؟ دائمی؟ ← حال ساده: I drink.
- الان؟ این هفته؟ موقت؟ در حال تغییر؟ ← حال استمراری: I'm drinking.

**فعل‌های حالت (state verbs):** know (دانستن)، understand (فهمیدن)، like (دوست داشتن)، love، hate، want (خواستن)، need (لازم داشتن)، believe، remember و belong معمولاً ing نمی‌گیرند، حتی برای «الان»: I know (نه I'm knowing)، I want a coffee now (نه I'm wanting).

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="I'm going to the gym every day." right="I go to the gym every day." why="every day = habit → present simple." />
<WrongRight wrong="She want a new phone." right="She wants a new phone." why="want is a state verb: present simple with -s." />
<WrongRight wrong="I'm knowing your brother." right="I know your brother." why="know is a state verb. No -ing." />
<WrongRight wrong="Listen! Somebody sings." right="Listen! Somebody is singing." why="Listen! = now → present continuous." />
<WrongRight wrong="What do you do now? I cook." right="What are you doing now? I'm cooking." why="For now, ask and answer with the present continuous." />
<WrongRight wrong="This month I stay with my aunt." right="This month I'm staying with my aunt." why="this month = temporary → present continuous." />

<MemoryHook title="Album or live video?">
Present simple is the **album**: what is always or usually true. Present continuous is a **live video**: what is happening now, just for now, or changing.
</MemoryHook>
