## Negatives and questions need do / does

<FormTable caption="Present simple: negative and question">

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|---|
| **−** | I **don't** work. | She **doesn't** work. |
| **?** | **Do** you work? | **Does** she work? |
| **Short answers** | Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**. | Yes, she **does**. / No, she **doesn't**. |

</FormTable>

The **-s** moves to **does**, so the main verb goes back to its base form: *She work**s**. → She **does**n't work. → **Does** she work?*

<Example context="social" fa="انگلیسی صحبت می‌کنی؟ بله.">“Do you speak English?” “Yes, I <mark>do</mark>.”</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="برادرم گوشت نمی‌خورد.">My brother <mark>doesn't eat</mark> meat.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="این مغازه جمعه‌ها باز است؟"><mark>Does</mark> this shop <mark>open</mark> on Fridays?</Example>

## Wh- questions

Put the question word first, then **do / does**, then the subject and the verb:

<FormTable caption="Question word + do / does + subject + verb">

| Question word | do / does | Subject | Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | **do** | you | live? |
| What | **does** | he | do? |
| When | **does** | the class | start? |

</FormTable>

<Example context="work" fa="شغل پدرت چیست؟">What <mark>does</mark> your father <mark>do</mark>?</Example>

<DecisionTree id="question-auxiliary" />

<Persian>

**منفی و سؤالی در حال ساده:** به یک فعل کمکی نیاز داریم: **do** (برای I / you / we / they) و **does** (برای he / she / it).

- منفی: I **don't** work. / She **doesn't** work.
- سؤالی: **Do** you work? / **Does** she work?

**مهم:** s فعل به does منتقل می‌شود، پس بعد از does / doesn't فعل اصلی **بدون s** است: Does she **work**? (نه works)

**اشتباه رایج فارسی‌زبان‌ها:** در فارسی فقط با لحن سؤال می‌پرسیم («کجا کار می‌کنی؟»)، پس «Where you work?» می‌گوییم. در انگلیسی do لازم است: **Where do you work?**

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="She doesn't likes coffee." right="She doesn't like coffee." why="No -s after doesn't." />

<MemoryHook title="The -s jumps to does">
In questions and negatives, the **-s jumps** from the verb to **do**: she work**s** → **does** she work? → she **does**n't work.
</MemoryHook>
