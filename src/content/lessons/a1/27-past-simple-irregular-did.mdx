## Irregular verbs

Many of the most common verbs don't add -ed. They have their **own past form**, and you have to learn it.

<Example context="travel" fa="تابستان گذشته به کیش رفتیم.">Last summer we <mark>went</mark> to Kish.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="دیروز یک گوشی جدید خریدم.">I <mark>bought</mark> a new phone yesterday.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="دیشب هشت ساعت خوابیدم.">I <mark>slept</mark> for eight hours last night.</Example>

The good news: like regular verbs, the irregular past is **the same for every subject**. *I went, she went, they went.* No -s.

## Irregular verbs in groups

It is easier to learn them in groups. Here are the most useful A1 verbs:

<FormTable caption="Common irregular verbs">

| Group | Base → past |
|---|---|
| **No change** | put → **put** · cost → **cost** · read → **read** (said /red/) |
| **Ends in -t** | sleep → **slept** · leave → **left** · meet → **met** · feel → **felt** · lose → **lost** · send → **sent** · spend → **spent** |
| **-ought / -aught** | buy → **bought** · bring → **brought** · think → **thought** · teach → **taught** |
| **Vowel change** | drink → **drank** · swim → **swam** · sing → **sang** · begin → **began** · come → **came** · get → **got** · sit → **sat** · run → **ran** · win → **won** |
| **Other changes** | go → **went** · see → **saw** · eat → **ate** · take → **took** · write → **wrote** · speak → **spoke** · drive → **drove** · know → **knew** · fly → **flew** |
| **Small changes** | have → **had** · make → **made** · do → **did** · say → **said** · pay → **paid** · tell → **told** · sell → **sold** · hear → **heard** · find → **found** · stand → **stood** |

</FormTable>

You'll find all of them, with their Persian meanings, in the [irregular verbs list](/reference#irregular). Learn the A1 list first. More irregular verbs come in A2 lesson 7 (Past simple: more irregular verbs).

The past of **be** is *was / were* (lesson 25). The past of **get** is *got*. (American English also has *gotten*, used after *have*: *I've gotten*. That comes with the present perfect, in A2 lesson 15, *Present perfect: experience*.)

## Negatives: didn't + base form

For **every** verb, regular or irregular, the negative is **didn't** (*did not*) + the **base form**. It is the same for every subject.

<FormTable caption="Past simple: negative">

| Positive | Negative | Full form (formal, written) |
|---|---|---|
| I **went** to work. | I **didn't go** to work. | I **did not go** to work. |
| She **saw** the film. | She **didn't see** the film. | She **did not see** the film. |
| They **worked** late. | They **didn't work** late. | They **did not work** late. |

</FormTable>

**did** already shows the past, so the main verb goes back to the base form: *didn't go*, not ~~didn't went~~.

<Example context="work" fa="دیروز سر کار نرفتم. مریض بودم.">I <mark>didn't go</mark> to work yesterday. I was sick.</Example>
<Example context="school" fa="او تکلیفش را انجام نداد.">He <mark>didn't do</mark> his homework.</Example>

## Yes/no questions and short answers

Put **Did** before the subject, and use the **base form**: **Did + subject + base form**.

<FormTable caption="Past simple: questions and short answers">

| Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| **Did** you **see** the match? | Yes, I **did**. | No, I **didn't**. |
| **Did** she **call** you? | Yes, she **did**. | No, she **didn't**. |
| **Did** they **enjoy** the trip? | Yes, they **did**. | No, they **didn't**. |

</FormTable>

In short answers, repeat **did**, not the main verb: *Yes, I did*, not ~~Yes, I saw~~.

<Example context="social" fa="«بازی را دیدی؟» «نه، ندیدم. زود خوابیدم.»">“<mark>Did</mark> you <mark>see</mark> the match?” “No, I <mark>didn't</mark>. I <mark>went</mark> to bed early.”</Example>
<Example context="health" fa="«دارویت را خوردی؟» «بله، خوردم.»">“<mark>Did</mark> you <mark>take</mark> your medicine?” “Yes, I <mark>did</mark>.”</Example>

## Wh- questions

Put the question word first: **question word + did + subject + base form**.

<FormTable caption="Wh- questions in the past">

| Question word | did | Subject | Base form | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What | **did** | you | **do** | yesterday? |
| Where | **did** | she | **go** | on vacation? |
| When | **did** | they | **arrive**? | |
| Who | **did** | you | **meet** | at the party? |
| Why | **did** | he | **leave** | early? |
| How | **did** | you | **get** | here? |
| What time | **did** | the film | **start**? | |

</FormTable>

Look at *What did you **do**?* Here **did** is the helper and **do** is the main verb (*I did my homework. I went shopping.*).

<Example context="work" fa="آخر هفته چه کار کردی؟">What <mark>did</mark> you <mark>do</mark> on the weekend?</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="کی به تهران رسیدی؟">When <mark>did</mark> you <mark>arrive</mark> in Tehran?</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="آن کفش‌ها را از کجا خریدی؟">Where <mark>did</mark> you <mark>buy</mark> those shoes?</Example>

## When we don't use did

- **With be.** The verb *be* makes its own negatives and questions: *I **wasn't** at home. **Were** you tired?* Not ~~I didn't be~~ or ~~Did you be~~. (See lesson 25.)
- **When who or what is the subject.** If the question word is the person or thing that did the action, use normal word order and the past form: **Who called** you? **What happened**? Compare: Who **did** you **call**? (you called someone). B1 lesson 1 (Subject & object questions) teaches this in full.

<Persian>

**فعل‌های بی‌قاعده:** بسیاری از فعل‌های پرکاربرد ed نمی‌گیرند و گذشته‌ی خاص خودشان را دارند: go ← went (رفتم)، see ← saw (دیدم)، buy ← bought (خریدم). این‌ها را باید حفظ کنید. بهتر است آن‌ها را **گروهی** یاد بگیرید (مثلاً buy / bought، think / thought، bring / brought). فهرست کامل با معنی فارسی در صفحه‌ی Reference هست. شکل گذشته برای همه‌ی فاعل‌ها یکسان است: I went، she went، they went.

**منفی با didn't:** در فارسی برای منفی کردن فقط «نـ» به اول همان فعل گذشته اضافه می‌کنیم: رفتم ← **نـ**رفتم. به همین دلیل فارسی‌زبان‌ها می‌گویند I didn't went (یعنی «نـ + رفتم»). اما در انگلیسی خودِ did زمان گذشته را نشان می‌دهد، پس فعل اصلی به شکل ساده برمی‌گردد:

- I went ← I **didn't go**. (نرفتم.)
- She saw it ← She **didn't see** it. (آن را ندید.)

**سؤالی با did:** در فارسی جمله‌ی سؤالی را فقط با لحن می‌سازیم: «فیلم را دیدی؟». در انگلیسی باید **Did** را اول جمله بیاوریم و فعل ساده شود: Did you see the film? (نه You saw the film? و نه Did you saw?)

**جواب کوتاه:** Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. در جواب، did را تکرار می‌کنیم، نه فعل اصلی را.

**سؤال با کلمه‌ی پرسشی:** کلمه‌ی پرسشی + did + فاعل + فعل ساده:

- Where did you go? (کجا رفتی؟) نه Where you went?
- What did you do? (چه کار کردی؟) اینجا did کمکی است و do فعل اصلی (انجام دادن).
- When did they arrive? (کی رسیدند؟)

**کجا did نمی‌آید؟**

- با فعل **be**: I wasn't at home. (خانه نبودم.) / Were you tired? (خسته بودی؟)
- وقتی who یا what **فاعل** است: Who called you? (چه کسی به تو زنگ زد؟) / What happened? (چه اتفاقی افتاد؟)

</Persian>

<MemoryHook title="did takes the past, the verb goes home">
When **did** arrives, it **takes the past**, and the main verb **goes back home** to its base form: did + go, did + see. No *did*? Then the verb keeps its own past: went, saw.
</MemoryHook>
