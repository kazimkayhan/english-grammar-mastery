## Irregular verbs

Many of the most common verbs don't add -ed. They have their own past form:

<FormTable caption="20 essential irregular verbs">

| Base | Past | Base | Past |
|---|---|---|---|
| go | **went** | come | **came** |
| see | **saw** | get | **got** |
| have | **had** | make | **made** |
| buy | **bought** | take | **took** |
| eat | **ate** | give | **gave** |
| drink | **drank** | find | **found** |
| write | **wrote** | think | **thought** |
| leave | **left** | know | **knew** |
| say | **said** | tell | **told** |
| sleep | **slept** | meet | **met** |

</FormTable>

You'll find all of them, grouped by level, in the [irregular verbs list](/reference#irregular). Learn the A1 list first.

<Example context="travel" fa="تابستان گذشته به کیش رفتیم.">Last summer we <mark>went</mark> to Kish.</Example>
<Example context="shopping" fa="دیروز یک گوشی جدید خریدم.">I <mark>bought</mark> a new phone yesterday.</Example>

## Negatives and questions: did

For **every** verb, regular or irregular, negatives and questions use **did**. The main verb goes back to the **base form**.

<FormTable caption="Past simple: negative and question">

| | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **−** | **didn't** + base form | I **didn't go** to work. |
| **?** | **Did** + subject + base form | **Did** you **go** to work? |
| **Short answers** | Yes, I **did**. / No, I **didn't**. | |
| **Wh- ?** | question word + **did** + subject + base form | Where **did** you **go**? |

</FormTable>

<Example context="social" fa="«بازی را دیدی؟» «نه، ندیدم. زود خوابیدم.»">“<mark>Did</mark> you <mark>see</mark> the match?” “No, I <mark>didn't</mark>. I <mark>went</mark> to bed early.”</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="آخر هفته چه کار کردی؟">What <mark>did</mark> you <mark>do</mark> at the weekend?</Example>

<Persian>

**فعل‌های بی‌قاعده:** بسیاری از فعل‌های پرکاربرد ed نمی‌گیرند و گذشته‌ی خاص خودشان را دارند: go ← went، see ← saw، buy ← bought. این‌ها را باید حفظ کنید (فهرست کامل در صفحه‌ی Reference).

**منفی و سؤالی با did:** برای همه‌ی فاعل‌ها و همه‌ی فعل‌ها:

- منفی: didn't + شکل ساده‌ی فعل ← I didn't go.
- سؤالی: Did + فاعل + شکل ساده ← Did you go?

**اشتباه بسیار رایج:** «I didn't saw» غلط است. did خودش زمان گذشته را نشان می‌دهد، پس فعل اصلی ساده می‌ماند: I didn't **see**.

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="She didn't came to class." right="She didn't come to class." why="didn't + base form." />
<WrongRight wrong="I goed to the park." right="I went to the park." why="go is irregular: went." />

<MemoryHook title="did takes the past, the verb goes home">
When **did** arrives, it **takes the past**, and the main verb **goes back home** to its base form: did + go, did + see.
</MemoryHook>
