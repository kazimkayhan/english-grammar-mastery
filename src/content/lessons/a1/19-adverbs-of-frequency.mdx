## From always to never

<FormTable caption="Adverbs of frequency">

| Adverb | How often | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **always** | 100% | I **always** have breakfast. |
| **usually** | about 90% | She **usually** walks to work. |
| **often** | about 70% | We **often** eat out. |
| **sometimes** | about 50% | They **sometimes** watch films. |
| **rarely** | about 10% | He **rarely** drinks coffee. |
| **never** | 0% | I **never** smoke. |

</FormTable>

## Where do they go?

- **Before the main verb**: *I <mark>often</mark> walk to work.*
- **After be**: *She is <mark>never</mark> late.*

<Example context="home" fa="همیشه قبل از خواب کتاب می‌خوانم.">I <mark>always</mark> read before bed.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="او معمولاً ساعت هشت به محل کار می‌رسد.">She <mark>usually</mark> gets to work at eight.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="ما هیچ‌وقت دیر نمی‌کنیم.">We are <mark>never</mark> late.</Example>

## How often?

<Example context="health" fa="هفته‌ای سه بار به باشگاه می‌روم.">I go to the gym <mark>three times a week</mark>.</Example>

- **every** day / week / year
- **once** a week (1×) · **twice** a month (2×) · **three times** a year (3×)

These longer phrases usually go at the **end** of the sentence.

<Persian>

**قیدهای تکرار:** always (همیشه)، usually (معمولاً)، often (اغلب)، sometimes (گاهی)، rarely (به‌ندرت)، never (هرگز).

**جای آن‌ها در جمله:**

- **قبل از فعل اصلی:** I often walk to work. (نه I walk often)
- **بعد از be:** She is never late.

**نکته:** never خودش منفی است، پس not نمی‌خواهد: I never smoke (نه I don't never smoke).

**How often…?** = هر چند وقت یک بار؟ جواب: every day (هر روز)، once a week (هفته‌ای یک بار)، twice a month (ماهی دو بار). این عبارت‌ها معمولاً آخر جمله می‌آیند.

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="I drink usually tea." right="I usually drink tea." why="Before the main verb." />
<WrongRight wrong="I don't never eat meat." right="I never eat meat." why="never is already negative." />

<MemoryHook title="Sit before the verb, stand after be">
Frequency adverbs **sit before** the action verb, but **stand after** be: I **often** walk · I **am** often tired.
</MemoryHook>
