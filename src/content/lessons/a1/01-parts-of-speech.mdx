## Every word has a job

Grammar is easier when you know what each word **does**. These jobs are called **parts of speech**.

<FormTable caption="The nine parts of speech">

| Part of speech | Its job | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| noun | names a person, place, thing or idea | Sara, Shiraz, phone, love |
| verb | an action or a state | go, eat, be, have |
| adjective | describes a noun | big, hot, happy |
| adverb | describes a verb: how, when, how often | slowly, today, always |
| pronoun | takes the place of a noun | I, you, he, she, it |
| preposition | shows place, time or direction | in, on, at, to |
| conjunction | joins words or sentences | and, but, or, because |
| determiner | comes before a noun | a, an, the, my, this |
| interjection | shows a feeling | Oh! Wow! Ouch! |

</FormTable>

<Example context="home" fa="مادرم چای داغ می‌نوشد." note="determiner · noun · verb · adjective · noun">My mother drinks hot tea.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="او با دقت کار می‌کند." note="pronoun · verb · adverb">She works carefully.</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="ما در تهران و شیراز هستیم." note="pronoun · verb · preposition · noun · conjunction · noun">We are in Tehran and Shiraz.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="وای! چه کیک خوبی!" note="interjection">Wow! What a nice cake!</Example>

## The parts of a sentence

A sentence has **parts**, too. The two you always need are a **subject** and a **verb**.

- **subject**: who or what the sentence is about. It comes **before** the verb: *<mark>Sara</mark> buys bread.*
- **verb**: what the subject does or is: *Sara <mark>buys</mark> bread.*
- **object**: who or what receives the action, **after** the verb: *Sara buys <mark>bread</mark>.*
- **complement**: describes the subject after *be*: *She is <mark>a doctor</mark>. She is <mark>happy</mark>.*
- **adverbial**: where, when or how: *Sara buys bread <mark>every morning</mark>.*

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="She is happily." right="She is happy." why="After be, describe the subject with an adjective, not an adverb." />
<WrongRight wrong="Ali tea drinks." right="Ali drinks tea." why="English order: subject + verb + object. The verb is not at the end." />
<WrongRight wrong="I like tea hot very." right="I like very hot tea." why="Adjectives come before the noun: hot tea." />

<MemoryHook title="Name it, do it, describe it">
**Nouns name** things. **Verbs do** things. **Adjectives describe nouns**, **adverbs describe verbs**. Everything else is glue.
</MemoryHook>
