## The empty it

In English, every sentence needs a subject. When there's no real subject (time, weather, distance, days, dates), we use **it**. This **it** means nothing; it just fills the subject place.

<FormTable caption="Empty it">

| Use | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Time | What time is **it**? | **It's** five o'clock. **It's** late. |
| Weather | What's the weather like? | **It's** cold. **It's** raining. |
| Distance | How far is **it** to the station? | **It's** two kilometers. **It's** not far. |
| Days and dates | What day is **it**? What's the date? | **It's** Monday. **It's** May 5. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="travel" fa="«تا فرودگاه چقدر راه است؟» «حدود بیست کیلومتر.»">“How far is <mark>it</mark> to the airport?” “<mark>It's</mark> about twenty kilometers.”</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="بیرون باران می‌آید. چترت را ببر."><mark>It's</mark> raining outside. Take your umbrella.</Example>
<Example context="work" fa="دیر است. بیا برویم خانه."><mark>It's</mark> late. Let's go home.</Example>

<Persian>

**«it» توخالی:** در فارسی برای زمان، هوا و فاصله فاعل نمی‌آوریم: «ساعت پنج است»، «سرد است»، «باران می‌آید». ولی جمله‌ی انگلیسی **همیشه فاعل** لازم دارد. در این موارد از **it** استفاده می‌کنیم که معنایی ندارد و فقط جای فاعل را پر می‌کند:

- زمان: It's five o'clock.
- هوا: It's cold. / It's raining.
- فاصله: It's two kilometers to the station.
- روز و تاریخ: It's Monday. / It's May 5.

**هرگز it را حذف نکنید:** «Is raining» غلط است ← It's raining.

</Persian>

## Common mistakes

<WrongRight wrong="Is very hot today." right="It's very hot today." why="A sentence needs a subject." />
<WrongRight wrong="There is cold today." right="It's cold today." why="Weather → it, not there." />

<MemoryHook title="it holds the seat">
Every English sentence has a **subject seat**. When nobody sits there (time, weather, distance), **it** holds the seat.
</MemoryHook>
