## The empty it

In English, every sentence needs a subject (lesson 3, *Every sentence needs a subject: SVO order*). When there's no real subject, as with time, weather, distance, days and dates, we use **it**. This **it** means nothing. It just fills the subject place.

<FormTable caption="Empty it">

| Use | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Time | What time is **it**? | **It's** five o'clock. **It's** late. |
| Weather | What's the weather like? | **It's** cold. **It's** raining. |
| Temperature | How hot is **it**? | **It's** 30 degrees. |
| Distance | How far is **it** to the station? | **It's** two kilometers. **It's** not far. |
| Days and dates | What day is **it**? What's the date? | **It's** Monday. **It's** May 5. |

</FormTable>

How to say the time (*half past nine, a quarter to six*) is in lesson 35, *Numbers, dates, times & prices*.

## Weather and temperature

Use **it** + **be** + an adjective, or **it** + a weather verb.

<FormTable caption="Talking about the weather">

| it + be + adjective | it + weather verb |
|---|---|
| It's **sunny** / **cloudy** / **windy**. | It's **raining**. (now) |
| It's **hot** / **warm** / **cold**. | It's **snowing**. (now) |
| It's **dark** / **light**. | It **rains** a lot in Rasht. (often) |
| It's **30 degrees**. | It **snowed** yesterday. (past) |

</FormTable>

<Example context="home" fa="بیرون باران می‌آید. چترت را ببر."><mark>It's</mark> raining outside. Take your umbrella.</Example>
<Example context="news" fa="امروز در تهران هوا ۳۵ درجه است."><mark>It's</mark> 35 degrees in Tehran today.</Example>
<Example context="home" fa="زمستان هوا خیلی زود تاریک می‌شود."><mark>It</mark> gets dark very early in winter.</Example>

To ask about the weather, say **What's the weather like?** Here *the weather* is the subject, so there is no *it*. You can also ask with *it*: **Is it cold outside?**

## Distance

<Example context="travel" fa="«تا فرودگاه چقدر راه است؟» «حدود بیست کیلومتر.»">“How far is <mark>it</mark> to the airport?” “<mark>It's</mark> about twenty kilometers.”</Example>
<Example context="travel" fa="ایستگاه دور نیست. با اتوبوس ده دقیقه راه است.">The station isn't far. <mark>It's</mark> ten minutes by bus.</Example>

- Ask: **How far is it** (from here) **to** the station?
- In everyday English, **far** is mostly used in questions and negatives: *How far is it? It's not far.* In a positive sentence, people usually say **a long way**: *It's a long way to the airport.*

## Days and dates

<Example context="work" fa="امروز دوشنبه است، پس موزه بسته است."><mark>It's</mark> Monday today, so the museum is closed.</Example>
<Example context="social" fa="امروز تولد من است!"><mark>It's</mark> my birthday today!</Example>

Ask: **What day is it** today? · **What's the date** today? How to say dates (*May 5, the fifth of May*) is in lesson 35.

## Questions and negatives

Empty **it** works like any other subject with **be** (lesson 5, *be: am, is, are*, and lesson 6, *be: questions & short answers*).

<FormTable caption="it with be">

| | Present | Past |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | **It's** cold. | **It was** cold. |
| Negative | **It isn't** cold. / **It's not** cold. | **It wasn't** cold. |
| Question | **Is it** cold? | **Was it** cold? |
| Short answer | Yes, **it is**. / No, **it isn't**. | Yes, **it was**. / No, **it wasn't**. |

</FormTable>

<Example context="travel" fa="«دور است؟» «نه، دور نیست.»">“<mark>Is it</mark> far?” “No, <mark>it isn't</mark>.”</Example>

With weather verbs, use the normal verb forms: *Is it raining? It isn't raining.* · *Does it snow here in winter?* (present simple questions, lesson 18).

## it or there?

**there is / there are** says that something exists or is in a place (lesson 14, *there is / there are*). **it** is for time, weather and distance.

<Example context="shopping" fa="نزدیک این‌جا یک داروخانه هست. دور نیست."><mark>There's</mark> a pharmacy near here. <mark>It</mark> isn't far.</Example>

So say **It's cold**, not ~~There is cold~~. More about *it* or *there*, and *It takes ten minutes* and *It's easy to learn*, comes in B1 lesson 4, *it or there?*

<Persian>

**«it» توخالی:** در فارسی برای زمان، هوا، فاصله و روز فاعل نمی‌آوریم: «ساعت پنج است»، «سرد است»، «باران می‌آید»، «امروز دوشنبه است». اما جمله‌ی انگلیسی **همیشه فاعل** لازم دارد. در این موارد از **it** استفاده می‌کنیم که معنایی ندارد و فقط جای فاعل را پر می‌کند:

- زمان: It's five o'clock. (ساعت پنج است.) / It's late. (دیر است.)
- هوا و دما: It's cold. (سرد است.) / It's 30 degrees. (هوا ۳۰ درجه است.) / It's dark. (تاریک است.)
- فاصله: It's two kilometers to the station. (تا ایستگاه دو کیلومتر است.)
- روز و تاریخ: It's Monday. (دوشنبه است.) / It's my birthday. (تولد من است.)

**«باران می‌آید»:** در فارسی می‌گوییم باران «می‌آید»، اما در انگلیسی rain خودش فعل است و فاعلش it است: **It's raining** (نه The rain comes). برف هم همین‌طور: It's snowing.

**هرگز it را حذف نکنید:** «Is raining» غلط است ← It's raining. همچنین برای هوا از there استفاده نکنید: There is cold ✗ ← It's cold.

**سؤال و منفی:** مثل هر فاعل دیگری با be: Is it cold? (سرد است؟) ← Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. · It isn't far. (دور نیست.) · گذشته: It was sunny yesterday. (دیروز آفتابی بود.)

**دور / راه طولانی:** far بیشتر در سؤال و منفی می‌آید: How far is it? (چقدر راه است؟) · It's not far. (دور نیست.) در جمله‌ی مثبت معمولاً می‌گوییم: It's a long way. (راه زیادی است.)

</Persian>

Every English sentence has a **subject seat**. When nobody sits there (time, weather, distance, days), **it** holds the seat.
